From the 15-bed “Tabors” to the state hospital

In 1887, one of the first psychiatric treatment institutions in Latvia was opened in Ģintermuiža, “Tabors” with 15 beds. In 1921 “Tabors” and the Ģintermuiža institutions were merged into the “Ģintermuiža” state hospital, and quiet detached-house streets lie around it.

This development is the reason Ģintermuiža appears in flood documents. In the LVĢMC flood risk maps of Jelgava, the Ģintermuiža (later hospital) development is named among the cultural-historical sites in the area at risk from spring floods, alongside the historic centre and the palace. The map speaks of this development, not of every street in the district.

A backflow valve in the outlet: when it is a requirement

Jelgava regulations No. 19-10 (point 23) state: if a property lies in the city’s flood plain and is connected to the centralised sewer, a backflow valve must be built into its sewer outlet. Whether your particular plot is in the flood plain, ask the Jelgava local authority or Jelgavas ūdens.

The Svēte has its own history in spring. In the report on the 2010 floods it is named as one of the first rivers in Jelgava to burst its banks, so the valve question is worth settling before the next spring, not during it.

A backflow valve is placed in the house outlet so that it can be reached for cleaning: a valve that is clogged or stuck half open gives no protection. If a house has a basement with a sink, shower or floor drain lower than the street chamber, find out before choosing a valve which outlets from the basement go to the street and which do not.

Backflow valve requirement: point 23 of Jelgava regulations No. 19-10, likumi.lv, checked on 19 November 2026.

The 2018 streets and the two parts of Gārņu iela

In phase V, section 10, the water supply and sewer in Stiebru iela, Smilgu iela, Sniega iela, Līgas iela, Niedru iela, Gārņu iela, Grīvas iela, Laipu iela and Dūņu iela were put into service at the end of 2018. If a plot in these streets is in the flood plain, the requirement of point 23 is met together with the connection, because then the outlet is open anyway.

In Gārņu iela the networks are not the same along its whole length: the water main was built along the whole street, but the sewer only from No. 8 to Bebru ceļš. For a house outside this sewer section, water can come from the city while wastewater still goes to the owner’s own system. For Palu iela, section 5 of the 2015 procurement for sections 1–6 provided for a gravity sewer.

Blocks of flats in Kastaņu iela and Kārklu iela

Our reading of the map (OpenStreetMap): blocks of flats are marked at Kastaņu iela 1, 2 and 2A and Kārklu iela 4. If a backflow valve or a basement pump is there, it is usually on the building’s shared outlet, and the flat owners or the building manager decide on it, unless the building’s documents say otherwise.

A flat owner on the ground floor or in a basement should know where the shared outlet valve is and whether it can be reached. This is checked together with the building manager before spring, not when the Svēte is already high.

Work in the basement: the pump and finding the fault

If water appears in a basement in spring floods, first find out where it comes from: the ground, rain, or back along the sewer outlet. That decides whether a backflow valve, a basement pump or both are needed. Finding the fault with no repair is from €25 per inspection; the price of fitting a basement or drainage pump depends on the amount of work, access and materials.

The work is described on the pages Basement and drainage pumps in Jelgava and Basement flooding in spring floods. It is a labour price, materials extra; on the call you go through the job and the price.

Questions people ask

Is the whole of Ģintermuiža in a flood zone?

The flood map names a specific development, not the district as a whole: in the LVĢMC flood maps of Jelgava, the Ģintermuiža (later hospital) development is named in the area at risk from spring floods. For a particular plot, ask the Jelgava local authority or Jelgavas ūdens.

In Gārņu iela the water main runs the whole length, but the sewer only from No. 8. What should a house outside that section do?

Such a house stays with its own sewage system, and it has to be registered in the Jelgava register of decentralised sewage systems at dks.jelgava.lv. About a possible connection in future, ask the Technical Department of Jelgavas ūdens.

Is a backflow valve needed even if the house has no basement?

Point 23 does not tie the requirement to a basement: it applies to a property in the flood plain that is connected to the centralised sewer.
