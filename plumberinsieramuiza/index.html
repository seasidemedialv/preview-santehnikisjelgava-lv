The official district Sieramuiža-Lediņi lies between the left bank of the Lielupe, the city’s eastern boundary along the Vircava, Lediņu ceļš, Bauskas iela and the Platone river. Sieramuiža also includes Miera and Staļģenes iela and Zemītes 1. līnija and 2. līnija.

Dzirnavu iela: a network since 2019

In section 18 of phase V, the water supply and sewer on Dzirnavu iela were put into operation in 2019. For a house on Dzirnavu iela, the way to the street pipe runs across the yard, and the yard pipe with its inspection chambers is the owner’s system.

If a house was connected soon after 2019, its yard pipe is as new as the street network. The house’s internal sewer can be older, and its joint with the new yard pipe is the place to look at first when water starts to drain more slowly.

Bauskas iela: a price survey is not construction

In 2026, Jelgavas ūdens held a price survey for extending the water supply and sewer networks on Bauskas iela. A survey is a preparatory step; whether and when the networks will be built is confirmed by the company.

On streets where there is no network yet, residents can propose building it themselves. Jelgava binding regulations No. 26-4, in force from 17 March 2026, provide that the municipality’s share can reach up to 70 %, while the residents' share must not be less than 30 %. Applications must be submitted by 10 March of the current year, and the networks built become the property of the municipal company.

When an allotment house becomes a home

The name Sieramuiža comes from the manor whose fields now hold allotments. If a garden house starts to be used all year round, three things change in the plumbing. The first is winter: Jelgavas ūdens advises keeping rooms with water supply and sewer pipes at least +5 °C and insulating the pipes.

The second is consumption. If a house is connected without a meter, Annex 1 of regulations No. 19-10 sets 215 litres a day per resident for a house with central water, sewerage, hot water, baths and showers; for a building without baths and showers 90 litres. The third is the amount of wastewater: a shower and a washing machine produce more water than a summer sink.

So if a bath or shower has been put into a garden house but the sewer is still the old one, first check whether the yard pipe or the tank can cope with that amount. Creating a new connection point is described on the page Plumbing first fix in a private house.

The Platone in spring floods and the yard pipe

The Platone is a left tributary of the Lielupe that joins the Lielupe in Jelgava, and in spring floods it is fast. The Lielupe basin plan, outside the nationally significant flood risk area, includes restoring the Platone river over 21.66 km. An LLU study links Jelgava’s flat terrain in general with high groundwater.

If sand and sediment build up in the inspection chambers, they are taken out before they block the pipe. Clearing the yard drain line costs from €50 (1st hour), high-pressure jetting from €60 an hour. The method is described on the page Sewer jetting in Jelgava.

Questions people ask

A shower has been put into a garden house in Sieramuiža. Will the old yard line cope?

An inspection of the line shows it: it is cleared and, if needed, looked at with a camera for a sagging section or a crack in the pipe. Clearing the yard drain line is from €50 per call-out (1st hour); the price of a camera look depends on the amount of work, access and materials.

Residents of Bauskas iela want a network on their section. How much can the municipality co-fund?

Under regulations No. 26-4 the municipality’s share can reach up to 70 %, and the residents' share is at least 30 %. An application must be submitted by 10 March of the year. In the assessment, extending both networks gets 20 points, extending one network 15.

An allotment house in Sieramuiža has become a permanent home. What has to change in the plumbing?

First the winter regime: Jelgavas ūdens advises keeping rooms with pipes at least +5 °C and insulating the pipes. If the house is connected without a meter, consumption is worked out by the norm, 215 l a day per resident in a house with baths and showers (Annex 1 of regulations No. 19-10).
