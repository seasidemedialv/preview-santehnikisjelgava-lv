Plumber in Pogu lauki - scattered houses with a long way to the street

Three streets, cut off from the rest of Jelgava by railways on two sides and by the Svēte on the third, make up Pogu lauki, about 3 km from the left bank of the Lielupe (our reading of the map).

The houses here are scattered and the setting is more like open country, so the route from the street network to the house can be long, and its price is set by the metres.

What was built in 2019 in section 29

Only three streets count as Pogu lauki: Lakstīgalu iela, Pogu lauku ceļš and Putnu iela. In section 29 of phase V, a water main, a gravity sewer, a sewage pumping station and a pressure main were built there, and also on Kārklu iela. The networks were put into operation in 2019.

Two routes to building the service pipe

The first route: the building specialists of SIA Jelgavas ūdens prepare a service pipe layout plan under Cabinet of Ministers Regulations No. 253. With it, an owner at whose boundary a street network branch has already been built can build the service pipe on their own land under a simplified procedure. The plan needs an application, land registry certificates and a copy of the boundary plan. The plan also shows exactly where the branch comes out at the boundary, and from that point the whole calculation for the long service pipe starts.

The second route: the company builds the service pipe itself. After an application it surveys the site, agrees the estimate and signs a contract, and after the works it prepares the documents for commissioning at the building authority. In this case, within two weeks of the acceptance certificate being signed, the contract must be concluded and you must become a subscriber.

Under the simplified procedure the deadline is different: the connection is accepted by a Jelgavas ūdens representative, and the service contract is concluded after acceptance. So before digging it is worth knowing which route you are taking, because the list of documents depends on it too.

The calculation: 25 m of water main and 25 m of sewer

Suppose the house is 25 m from the boundary and both pipes run in one trench, at the distance between them set in the design. The labour part can be worked out from the price lines:

Water pipe
25 m × €10/m = at least €250.
Sewer pipe
25 m × €10/m = at least €250.
Inlet and outlet in the house
2 connection points × €35 = at least €70.
Total for labour
at least €570, materials extra.

Depth is a decision made before digging. The depth of the water main is set by the LBN 003-19 frost depth table, which we explain further on the page Plumber in Dalbe; where that cannot be observed, for example when crossing a path or foundations, the section is insulated. The sewer pipe needs an even fall along its whole length, so on a long run its depth at the house and at the boundary differ.

Across Jelgava, house service pipes total 47,501 m of the 250,258 m water network (1 January 2026), so almost every fifth metre of water main in the city is someone’s service pipe. These are metres of the Jelgavas ūdens network; the yard section from the branch at the boundary to the house is the owner’s building work. Everything about the connection is on the page Sewer and water connection for a house.

A pumping station in the street, a gravity drain in the yard

In section 29 a sewage pumping station with a pressure main was also built in the street. It is part of the street network: the street’s gravity sewer collects the wastewater, and the station pumps it onward. The city has 32 such stations, because on flat terrain the sewer would not work without them.

A house’s yard drain is usually gravity-fed to the branch at the boundary, and its height is set by the branch level in the service pipe layout plan. On a long run it is exactly this level that decides how deep the pipe ends up at the house. You need your own lifting pump only if some fixture in the house is lower than it.

Questions people ask

A house on Pogu lauku ceļš will have a 40 m service pipe. Build it myself or order from Jelgavas ūdens?

If the branch to the boundary already exists, with a service pipe layout plan the service pipe on your own land can be built under the simplified procedure. If Jelgavas ūdens builds it, the company surveys the site, agrees the estimate and prepares the documents for the building authority. The choice comes down to price and how much of the paperwork the owner wants to take on. With a 40 m service pipe, the pipe laying alone is at least €400 per pipe (40 m × €10/m), materials extra.

Jelgavas ūdens is building the service pipe. What has to be done after the acceptance certificate?

If the company builds the service pipe, within two weeks of the acceptance certificate being signed the service contract must be concluded and you must become a subscriber. If you build the service pipe yourself under the simplified procedure, the contract is concluded after the company’s representative has accepted the connection.

In Pogu lauki there is a sewage pumping station in the street. Will my yard need a pump too?

Usually not. The station is part of the street network, and a house’s outlet to the branch at the boundary is usually gravity-fed. You need your own pump only if some fixture in the house, for example in the basement, is lower than the branch.
