The street networks in Jaunais gals and Dienvidu gals were built in different years, so what applied to neighbours further north may not apply to your house.

The main difference for plumbing is time: the Tērvetes iela network in Jaunais gals was handed over about two years earlier than in Dienvidu gals. Dienvidu gals is counted as 20 streets, including Aku ceļš, Baložu iela, Krastmalas iela, Palienas iela, Plostu iela and Sprieguma iela, and not all of them are mentioned in section 31.

A branch may reach the boundary, but not on every plot

For houses in Plostu iela, Tērvetes iela and Baložu iela, the branch may reach the boundary; Jelgavas ūdens clarifies this by address.

Point 20 of Jelgava binding regulations No. 19-10 states that the owner is responsible for operating the water supply and sewer system of their property and stops leaks in it at their own expense. The yard pipe, the garden tap and the service pipe into the house are therefore the owner’s responsibility.

If a house has so far taken water from its own well and is now connecting to the Tērvetes iela network but keeps the well for the garden, the two systems must stay separate; how that is done is explained on the page Plumber in Ozolpils.

A garden tap and a separate meter for watering

In small gardens water often goes into the ground rather than into the sewer. Jelgavas ūdens installs for its subscribers an additional meter for water that is not discharged into the sewer, for example for watering a garden. This meter is sealed only by the company’s authorised staff.

The plumber’s part is the branch from the house’s water pipe to the outer wall and the outdoor tap itself. The branch is made so that there is room for the additional meter before the garden tap. A tap through the wall is priced by the amount of work, access and materials, while every new connection point in the house starts from €35.

In autumn the outdoor tap and its branch are worth draining. How to prepare the whole system for winter is described on the page Winterising the water system of a garden house or summer cottage.

A leak in a long garden pipe

On a long detached-house plot, the water pipe to the house and the branch to the garden can be tens of metres long, and a leak in the ground is often not visible. The first sign is usually a meter that turns when all the taps are closed.

Point 21 of regulations No. 19-10 requires the owner to report leaks in their own system to the water service provider without delay, and point 20 makes the owner stop them at their own expense. Stopping a drip or leak point starts from €40 per job; if a section of pipe has to be replaced, the price is set by the metres. How it is done is described on the page Leak and pipe repair in Jelgava.

Flood protection measures for the Tērvetes iela area in the plan

In the Lielupe basin flood management plan, “Comprehensive flood protection measures in the Tērvetes iela area” is included as a priority 2 measure. It is a list in a plan, not an announcement of works or their timing; whether the measures affect your street, ask the Jelgava local authority.

Our reading of the map (OpenStreetMap): Aku ceļš, Apoga iela and Asnu iela are 4–6 km from the Lielupe. This distance applies to these three streets, not to the whole district.

Questions people ask

I want a second meter for watering the garden. Who installs it?

An additional meter for water that does not go into the sewer is installed for its subscribers by Jelgavas ūdens, and only its authorised staff seal it. The plumber can make a branch to the outer wall and the outdoor tap, leaving room for the meter.

In a house in Aku ceļš the meter turns when all the taps are closed. Where should I look for the leak?

First in the sections that cannot be seen: the service pipe from the boundary to the house and the branch to the garden tap. If the garden branch can be shut off separately, close it and see whether the meter stops; that shows on which side the leak is. Stopping a drip is from €40 per job.

In Plostu iela the networks were handed over in 2020. Why is a neighbour's connection in Jaunais gals older?

The northern part of Tērvetes iela in Jaunais gals was in phase V, section 24, handed over at the end of 2018. Plostu iela, Tērvetes iela and Baložu iela in Dienvidu gals were in section 31, handed over at the end of 2020.
