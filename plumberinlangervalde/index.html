Plumber in Langervalde - 27 hectares of houses by the old park forest

The forest of the former Langervalde park takes up most of this district in the east of Jelgava on the Lielupe’s right bank, while detached houses and, in places, industrial buildings stand along the roads.

The street networks here were planned and built street by street in different years, so the connection status of neighbouring houses may differ, and the switch from a septic tank to the street network starts with your street’s section.

1960s buildings between the railway and the forest

In the 1960s a 27 ha private-house area was laid out in Pārlielupe between the railway line and the Langervalde forest. In the division under the residents' council regulations, Langervalde lies between the city’s eastern boundary, the right bank of the Lielupe and the Jelgava–Krustpils and Jelgava–Riga railway lines. Our map reading (OpenStreetMap): Langervaldes iela and Alkšņu, Apiņu and Biešu streets are about 0.8–1.2 km from the Lielupe.

In a house built before the street sewer, the outlet usually leads to a septic tank or holding tank in the yard. Once a network appears in the street, this outlet is redirected to the new branch, and the first question is whether the old yard line can be used or has to be laid anew.

Street by street: what was planned in 2013, 2015 and 2020

Jelgavas ūdens tenders and reports mention Langervalde streets three times. A tender document shows what was planned; handover into operation is confirmed only by the 2020 report:

  • Alkšņu iela: the 2013 Phase III tender provided for gravity sewerage.
  • Parka, E. Melngaiļa, Apiņu, Rasas, Brūkleņu and Egļu streets: section 4 of the 2015 procurement for sections 1–6 provided for gravity sewerage and a water main.
  • Rubeņu ceļš and Jaunais ceļš: in Phase V section 8 the water main and sewer were put into operation in the second quarter of 2020.

A landmark for Rubeņu ceļš and Langervaldes iela is Loka maģistrāle: when it was rebuilt in 2018–2020, roundabouts were built at both junctions. “Gravity” means wastewater flows to the street branch by itself, so the house outlet needs a fall along its whole length down to the branch level at the boundary.

The old yard line to the septic tank: reuse it or lay a new one

If part of the existing line is to be used for the new connection, it is first cleared and looked at with a camera. That shows whether the pipe has cracks, deposits or a sagging section that would cope worse on the new network than it did with the septic tank.

If the line is not usable, the new section is laid from the house outlet to the boundary. The plumber’s job is the house side: the outlet, the switch-over in the house and the yard section, which on the call is assessed by length and the amount of digging. We describe the course of the work on the page Mains sewer and water connection for a private house.

While the house still runs on a septic tank

Since 1 March 2021 the municipality has delegated to Jelgavas ūdens the inspection of all of the city’s decentralised sewage systems. For a Langervalde house still running on a septic tank, this means the tank must be registered and in a condition that passes the inspection until the outlet is switched over to the street network.

If the septic tank stays, its emptying and the condition of the yard line are checked together: if water in the house starts to drain slowly, the cause can be either a full tank or a blocked line between the house and the tank.

Switching over to the branch: an example for a house on Rubeņu ceļš

Suppose the old line to the septic tank can be used in part: it is cleared, looked at with a camera, and from the septic tank’s place to the branch at the boundary 8 m of new pipe with one new connection point has to be laid. The labour part is at least €165: clearing the line from €50, 8 m × €10/m = €80 and 1 × €35, plus a camera look according to the amount of work.

Digging and materials are not included in this sum; on the call you go through the job and the price according to the length of the route. The location and depth of the branch at the boundary are given by the technical specifications, and the depth at which the new pipe comes out at the septic tank’s place depends on that.

Questions people ask

A house on Rubeņu ceļš is being switched from a septic tank to the street branch. What does the labour price consist of?

Clearing the line (from €50 per call-out), a camera look according to the amount of work, metres of new pipe (from €10/m) and connection points (from €35 per outlet). The example with 8 m of pipe is higher up the page: at least €165 for the work, digging and materials extra.

After the septic tank was emptied, water in the house still drains slowly. Where is the cause?

In the line between the house and the tank. If the tank was emptied recently but water still stands, the line is cleared and, if the blockage keeps coming back, looked at with a camera. Clearing the yard drain line is from €50 per call-out (1st hour).

Since which year has Jelgavas ūdens inspected septic tanks in Langervalde too?

Since 1 March 2021. The municipality has delegated to Jelgavas ūdens the inspection of all of the city’s decentralised sewage systems, which means Langervalde’s septic tanks and holding tanks too.
