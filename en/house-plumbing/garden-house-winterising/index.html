Frost in clay 59–119 cm and the allotment districts

LBN 003-19 (table 15) gives clay soil in Jelgava a standard frost depth of 59 cm for a winter that recurs once in two years, 91 cm once in ten years and 119 cm once in a hundred years; in sandy soil it is more. A garden pipe laid shallower must be left empty in winter.

Jelgava has whole districts where allotments alternate with detached houses. Būriņu dārzi is separated from the rest of the city by the Svēte, Sieramuiža by the Platone, and Lediņi is an allotment district by the Vircava. The lanes off Būriņu ceļš are often narrow and overgrown, so access to the inlet and the garden house in autumn affects the amount of work. On 12 March 2026 Bāra ceļš flooded and was closed, so spring opening there sometimes has to start with a look at the basement.

The situation in the municipality is similar: on 5 March 2026 LSM reported that the chair of Jelgava Municipality Council named, among the most vulnerable places, spots on the Jelgava side of the municipality where allotments have been turned into residential zones, and Ūziņi by the Svēte, which can overflow its banks within three hours.

Jelgava's winter figures for a garden house

Frost depth in clay
LBN 003-19 (table 15) for Jelgava: 59 cm in a winter that recurs once in two years, 79 cm once in five, 91 cm once in ten years. Water is not left for winter in a pipe laid shallower.
The coldest five days
on average -21.7 °C, absolute minimum -34.9 °C in February (LBN 003-19, tables 3 and 6).
Average minimum of the coldest month
-10.3 °C (table 5); at such a temperature the water in a trap and a toilet cistern in an unheated house can freeze.
A room with pipes
at least +5 °C, pipes insulated if the water is not drained (Jelgavas ūdens advice).
Water heater
Bosch (Tronic 2000T) requires a room where the temperature does not fall below 0 °C; in an unheated house the water heater is drained.
Drainage pump, if it stays in the pit
fully submerged, liquid and surroundings at least +3 °C (Wilo-Drain TMW 32).

Water heater, traps and toilet cistern: empty in autumn, full in spring

A water heater holds tens of litres, and if the room falls below 0 °C, ice expands in the tank and the safety group; the Bosch Tronic 2000T instructions therefore require a room above zero. A water heater is drained for winter following its instructions, and it is disconnected from the power first. Traps and the toilet cistern must also be left empty, because the average minimum of the coldest month in Jelgava is -10.3 °C.

Opening the water system in spring goes in the opposite order: the drain taps are closed, the inlet is opened slowly, the water heater is filled first and only then switched on, and every joint is checked, because frost tends to burst exactly those. A watering meter is installed by Jelgavas ūdens for its subscribers and sealed only by the company’s authorised staff, so ask the company before removing or moving it.

The decentralised sewage system register in the city and the municipality: for saunas and summer cottages too

Plumbing in a summer cottage often ends in a holding tank or a septic tank, and the water supply to a sauna or a garden shower is then part of the decentralised sewerage. Jelgavas ūdens states that not only residential houses but also summer cottages, summer houses and saunas must be registered, even a dry toilet in an allotment. A new system in the city is registered within 1 month of the start of use (Regulations No. 19-17), in the system dks.jelgava.lv.

In the municipality’s villages, for example Brankas or Kalnciems, septic tanks and holding tanks are covered by municipality regulations No. 5 (2021), and the register is kept by Jelgavas novada KU, which registers systems free of charge.

Questions people ask

How deep does the ground freeze in Jelgava at a garden house pipe?

For clay soil in Jelgava LBN 003-19 gives 59 cm for a winter recurring every two years and 91 cm for one recurring every ten; in sandy soil it is more. A pipe shallower than that must be left empty in winter.

May I remove the watering meter for winter myself?

It is installed by Jelgavas ūdens and sealed only by the company’s authorised staff. Before removing it, ask the company what to do about the seal.

Can a drainage pump stay in the pit all winter?

The Wilo-Drain TMW 32 instructions allow it if the pump is fully submerged and the liquid and surroundings are not colder than +3 °C. If it is not used for a long time, one cycle must be run every 2 months; otherwise the pump is taken out.
