The plumber builds the house side: the outlet from the house to the boundary, the water service pipe to the meter unit and the switch-over inside. The connection to the street network itself is entrusted by the regulations to Jelgavas ūdens or a licensed contractor under its representative’s supervision.

83.49 km of new networks in 100 street sections

In the project “Development of Water Management Services in Jelgava, Phase V”, water supply and sewer networks were built and rebuilt over 83.49 km in 100 street sections. After it, the availability of sewerage services in the agglomeration reached 99.9% and of water supply in the zone 99.5%. A pipe laid in the street does not by itself connect a house: the owner builds or orders the yard section to the house.

For example, in section 25 of Phase V in Viskaļi (Ruļļu iela, Liepājas iela, Platones iela, Vizbuļu iela and others) the networks were put into operation in the third quarter of 2020, and in section 16 in Romas krogs (Dainas iela, Skuju iela, Turaidas iela) at the end of 2018. In 2026 Jelgavas ūdens held price enquiries for network expansion at Vītolu ceļš and Bauskas iela.

Connecting continues. In the local government’s report for 2025, 293 contracts were concluded with private individuals, 78 of them for new connections, while by February 2026 the register of decentralised systems held 3,661 entries.

Switching from a septic tank to centralised sewerage

When a house gives up its septic tank, the outlet is usually rerouted: the old pipe led to the tank, the new one goes to the boundary. Regulations No. 19-10 (sub-point 15.7) prohibit discharging precipitation and groundwater into household sewerage at will, so a roof downpipe or drainage that was joined to the old outlet is separated.

Jelgavas ūdens looks for them with a smoke generator, and for drainage without construction documents it can calculate compensation by the pipe’s internal diameter.

A backflow valve in the outlet in a flood-prone area

Point 23 of Regulations No. 19-10: if a property is in the flood-prone area of Jelgava city and is connected to centralised sewerage, a backflow valve must be built into its outlet. On the LVĢMC flood risk maps the places at risk from spring floods include the historic centre and the Ģintermuiža buildings, but the boundary of the flood-prone area is set by the local government, and for a specific plot you ask it. The backflow valve is fitted at the time of connection, in a place where it can be opened and cleaned.

A water service pipe to the meter, and nothing before it

Regulations No. 19-10 (sub-points 15.8. and 15.9) prohibit making internal water pipe connections before the commercial metering unit and combining local water supply with centralised; the two systems are separated by an air gap. If the house has had its own water supply until now, it stays separate. For garden watering Jelgavas ūdens installs an additional meter for its subscribers, and only its authorised staff seal it.

In Ozolnieki and Kalnciems the boundary is the first inspection chamber

Outside the city, Jelgava Municipality binding regulations No. 2 (from 9 September 2025) apply. Under point 20, the responsibility boundary for sewerage is the first inspection chamber on the outlet, counting from the service provider’s network, and for a detached house’s water pipe the property boundary. If raised groundwater at the boundary could damage the meter, sub-point 21.2 allows the unit to be built in another agreed place. The builder of the Ozolnieki networks noted that groundwater there had to be lowered continuously. In the municipality, co-financing for a sewer connection is governed by municipality regulations No. 26 (2022): 50% of the costs, but no more than €1,500, excluding the building’s internal sewerage.

If there is no network in the street yet: a residents' application by 10 March

Jelgava regulations No. 26-4 (in force from 17 March 2026) allow residents to propose a new water main or sewer in their own street. The local government’s share can reach 70%, the residents' share is not less than 30%, and applications are accepted until 10 March of the current year. In the scoring, extending both networks gets 20 points, one network 15, extension with rebuilding 10, and rebuilding only 5.

Example: a detached house on Ruļļu iela in Viskaļi

The street network there was put into operation in 2020. If it is 18 m from the boundary to the house and both pipes go in the trench, that is 36 m at from €10/m, at least €360; digging the trench is not included in this price and is charged separately. Inside, 2 connection points are switched over at from €35, at least €70. In total at least €430 for the work, materials extra; the plumber goes through the camera look and the trip on the call before the job.

Questions people ask

Can technical conditions be obtained in one year and the yard dug in the following one?

Yes, if you stay within the time limit. Regulations No. 19-10 (point 8) say that issued technical conditions are valid for 2 years.

Is co-financing granted if the pipe in the yard has already been laid?

No. Sub-point 5.3 of Regulations No. 19-2 excludes support if the connection has already been built or construction began before the application. So the application is submitted before the work.

Why does acceptance need a CCTV inspection report on the sewer?

Point 12 of Regulations No. 19-10 requires it: for a new or rebuilt gravity sewer network, a CCTV inspection of the pipeline before handover, and for a water main a hydraulic test and disinfection with a certificate.
