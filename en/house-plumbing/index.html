Yard trench, inlet room, water heater, purchase and garden house

The yard and the plot boundary. This is where the outlet to the street network and the water service pipe run. In the city, connection to the street network is made by Jelgavas ūdens or a licensed contractor under its representative’s supervision (Regulations No. 19-10, point 11), but the yard section is the owner’s responsibility. On this: sewer and water connection for a private house.

The inlet and the utility room. A meter unit inside a room may only be where the air is not cooler than +5 °C and no further than 2 m from the outer wall (point 13). All internal water piping starts from this place, which a new build lays from scratch: plumbing installation in a private house.

Kitchen, bathroom, water heater. These points are reviewed in the annual service; for example, for a Bosch (Tronic 2000T) water heater the manufacturer requires the anode to be checked every year. A yearly plan for the whole house: plumbing maintenance in a private house.

The whole house before a deal. On becoming the owner, within 5 working days you must submit to Jelgavas ūdens a handover report with the meter reading or conclude a contract (point 38). What to measure before signing: plumbing check before selling or buying.

A garden house and a sauna. In clay soil in Jelgava the ground freezes to 59 cm once in two years (LBN 003-19), so in an unheated house the water is drained for winter: winterising a garden house or summer cottage water system. Basement water and pumps have their own section: drainage and basement pumps.

3,661 systems in the register and 78 new connections in 2025

In Jelgava many private houses still use their own sewerage. By February 2026 the local government’s register of decentralised systems held 3,661 entries, of which 128 were added in 2025. In that year Jelgavas ūdens inspected 534 systems, and 324 did not meet the requirements of the regulations.

At the same time the city is connecting to networks built in the Phase V project: 83.49 km of water main and sewerage in 100 street sections. In 2025 293 contracts were concluded with private individuals, 78 of them for new connections. That is why private house jobs in Jelgava often start at the boundary, not at the tap.

The older houses keep their history. Jelgavas ūdens writes that until 1882 the city used water from the Svēte, brought through bored wooden pipes, and from 1882 water was distributed through a cast iron pressure main. In 1944 almost 90% of the city was destroyed, and some of the 18th- and 19th-century timber houses have survived around Vecpilsētas iela.

Ozolnieki, Brankas, Mežciems: municipality regulations and their own networks

Outside the city boundary, Jelgava Municipality binding regulations No. 2 (from 9 September 2025) apply, and the networks are operated by Jelgavas novada KU. The company’s water tests list separate systems, for example Ozolnieki, Brankas, Āne and Tetele, and Jaunpēternieki. In Brankas, at “Viskaļi” and “Grantiņi”, the street surface is laid after the connections to the networks. In the Āne detailed plan, at Celtnieku iela 11B, the water main and sewerage are to be built in the second phase, so there the internal plumbing of a new build is sometimes done before the street network.

In Ozolnieki a Cohesion Fund project was to build 6.6 km of water main, 6.3 km of sewerage and 5 pumping stations, with new sewer connections for more than 200 households on 21 streets. In Mežciems and Kārniņi water is taken from a borehole, and according to LVĢMC data for 2014 it was supplied untreated; the municipality’s environmental report shows centralised sewerage with biological treatment plants in both villages.

A yard tap and a watering meter

Garden water in Jelgava has a rule point of its own. Sub-point 15.8 of Regulations No. 19-10 prohibits any internal water pipe connection before the meter unit, so a watering branch too. For water that does not end up in the sewer, Jelgavas ūdens installs an additional meter for its subscribers, and it is sealed only by the company’s authorised staff. An outdoor tap on the wall is taken off after the meter and drained for winter.

Example: a house in Miezīte after the street network was built

In sections 22 and 23 of Phase V in Miezīte (Pūra, Kūliņu, Žagaru ceļš and others) the networks were put into operation in 2020. If a house is 15 m from the boundary to the wall and both pipes go in the trench, that is 30 m at from €10/m, so at least €300; digging the trench is not included in this price, you go through it separately. Inside, 3 connection points are switched over at from €35, at least €105. In total at least €405 for the work, materials extra; whether the trip is charged separately you find out on the call with the plumber before the job.

Questions people ask

Do Jelgava City regulations No. 19-10 apply in Ozolnieki and Kalnciems?

No. City regulations No. 19-10 and 19-17 apply to the city of Jelgava, while in the municipality Jelgava Municipality binding regulations No. 2 are in force. In them the responsibility boundary for sewerage is the first inspection chamber on the outlet, counting from the service provider’s network.

How many litres a day do the regulations assume for a detached house without a meter?

Annex 1 of Regulations No. 19-10 assumes 215 litres a day per resident for a detached house with centralised water, sewerage, hot water, baths and showers, and 90 litres for a building without baths and showers.

Why does Jelgava's sewerage need pumping stations?

The city is flat, so a gravity network alone cannot cope. Jelgavas ūdens states that 32 sewage pumping stations have been built in the city, and all work automatically.
