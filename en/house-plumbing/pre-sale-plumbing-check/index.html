Plumbing check before selling - meter, backflow valve and documents

A plumbing check before selling in Jelgava gives both sides the same facts: the meter reading and the place of the meter unit, a backflow valve in the outlet, the condition of the outlet and the status of the sewage system.

5 working days for the new owner

Point 38 of Jelgava regulations No. 19-10: on becoming the owner or holder of a property, within 5 working days one must submit to Jelgavas ūdens a handover act with the meter reading, an application for disconnection, or conclude a contract. The reading is therefore usually taken on the day of the deal, and during the check it is worth making sure that the meter unit is accessible and the seal is in place.

If the house is not connected to the city sewerage, there is a second question: whether the septic tank or holding tank is registered. Jelgavas ūdens states that registration is needed for summer houses, saunas and storage buildings too, and failing to register is an administrative offence. The status can be seen in the system dks.jelgava.lv; before a deal it is usually asked of the seller.

The flood-prone area: the backflow valve and the drainage pressure pipe

On the LVĢMC Jelgava flood risk maps the areas at risk from spring floods include the historic centre, Jelgava Palace with its park and the Ģintermuiža buildings; the map shows the risk, but the area under the regulations is specified by the local government. If the house being bought is in the city’s flood-prone area and connected to sewerage, point 23 of Regulations No. 19-10 requires a backflow valve in the outlet.

In the basement it is worth looking at the pressure pipe of the drainage pump. If it has been led into household sewerage without approval, sub-point 15.7 of the regulations prohibits this, and after the purchase the question stays with the new owner.

A purchase in Ozolnieki or Kalnciems: the municipality's procedure

Outside the city boundary Jelgava Municipality regulations No. 2 apply and water is supplied by Jelgavas novada KU, so whether a meter unit exists and where it is, is the first thing checked.

For septic tanks and holding tanks the villages of the municipality have their own regulations No. 5 (2021), and the register is kept by Jelgavas novada KU, which registers systems free of charge. Before a deal, ask the seller whether the system is registered there and which contractor empties it.

Example: a detached house in Ģintermuiža before a sale

On the LVĢMC map the Ģintermuiža buildings are among the objects at risk from floods; if the house is in the city’s flood-prone area, the check should include the backflow valve. Suppose that looking at the meter unit, the backflow valve and the basement takes 2 hours at from €25/h: at least €50 for the job. A camera look at the outlet is charged after the inspection, and the plumber goes through the trip on the call before the job.

Questions people ask

When is the meter reading taken if a house is sold?

Usually on the day of the deal, because the new owner must submit to Jelgavas ūdens within 5 working days a handover act with the reading or conclude a contract (Regulations No. 19-10, point 38).

How do I find out whether a house is in Jelgava's flood-prone area?

For a specific plot, ask the local government (the building authority) for the boundary. The LVĢMC Lielupe basin flood risk maps show the spring flood risk in Jelgava city, but that is a different document; if the house is in the flood-prone area under the regulations, the outlet needs a backflow valve.

Does an unregistered septic tank become the buyer's concern?

The duty to register lies with the system’s owner, and after the deal that is the new owner. Unless something else is specified, before the contract it is usually agreed who registers the system at dks.jelgava.lv.
