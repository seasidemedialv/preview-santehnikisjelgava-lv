534 systems inspected and 324 non-compliances

Since 1 March 2021 the local government has delegated to Jelgavas ūdens the inspection of all the city’s decentralised sewage systems. In 2025 the company inspected 534 systems, and 324 of them did not meet the requirements of the regulations. For a private house with a septic tank or a biological unit, maintenance is therefore not just a habit: it leaves a trace in the documents.

Connected houses have their own check too. Jelgavas ūdens uses a smoke generator to look for places where rain, ground or drainage water enters household sewerage, and for unauthorised connections. In the annual service it is worth making sure that nothing of the kind is joined to the house outlet.

Deadlines and measurements from the regulations and instructions

Emptying the septic tank
at least once a year (Jelgava regulations No. 19-17, point 14); for a holding tank the frequency is calculated by the formula A = B/C, but not less often than once a year.
Act for a factory-made treatment unit
by 1 April for the maintenance done in the previous year (sub-point 3.1.2).
Drainage pump when idle
one pumping cycle every 2 months, a service check after 1,500 operating hours (Wilo-Drain TMW 32).
Rooms with pipes in winter
at least +5 °C, pipes insulated (Jelgavas ūdens advice).
Pressure at the inlet
Jelgava regulations No. 19-10 provide for at least 0.26 MPa; if the pressure gauge shows much less, the cause is looked for in the inlet or the filter.

Annual plumbing check and drainage in the outlet

If a drainage system is joined to the house outlet without construction documentation, or the contract does not provide for discharging groundwater, Jelgavas ūdens can calculate and recover compensation; the amount depends on the pipe’s internal diameter. The annual check therefore traces where the drainage pump’s pressure pipe ends up. For its TMW 32 Wilo requires a pressure pipe with a gate valve and a backflow valve, protected from frost.

House plumbing check after the flood season

Point 23 of Jelgava regulations No. 19-10 requires a backflow valve in the sewer outlet for properties in the city’s flood-prone area. After spring floods it is opened and cleaned, because a flap left half-closed also holds back the house’s waste water. Wilo prohibits dry running of the pump: if a pump has run in a dry pit, before the following spring it should be checked that the seal has not been damaged.

Questions people ask

Does a septic tank need emptying once a year even if only one person lives in the house?

Jelgava regulations No. 19-17 (point 14) call for a septic tank to be pumped out at least yearly, however many people live in the house. Emptying is a sludge removal service; the plumber looks at the outlet and the pipe up to the tank.

By what date must the maintenance act for a factory-made treatment unit be submitted?

Jelgava regulations No. 19-17 require the owner to submit by 1 April an act on the maintenance done in the previous year (sub-point 3.1.2). For the septic tank itself the regulations require emptying at least once a year (point 14).

What should be checked in the meter room before winter?

Regulations No. 19-10 (point 13) allow the commercial metering unit in a room where the air is not colder than +5 °C, the place is accessible and no further than 2 m from the outer wall. The service checks that this is still so in the room and that the pipes are insulated.
