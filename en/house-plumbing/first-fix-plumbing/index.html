From there the internal water pipes, the sewerage and the connections for the kitchen, bathroom, toilet and appliances are built. The new-build price per outlet is from €55, labour price, materials extra.

The utility room's place: the decision that determines everything else

The first choice to make before building is the place of the utility room. It must be by an outer wall, because the city regulations No. 19-10 (point 13) do not allow the unit further than 2 m from the wall, and it must stay warm all winter. From it the water pipe distribution goes in one direction, and the soil stack with the outlet through the foundations in the other. The shorter the runs to the kitchen and bathroom, the fewer metres.

Detailed plans where the networks come in a second phase

Around Jelgava, new detached-house areas do not always get networks at the same time as the houses. In Brankas the detailed plan “Viskaļi”, “Grantiņi” (2023) provides for the street surface to be built in the second phase, after the connections to centralised networks, once they are nearby. In Āne the detailed plan at Celtnieku iela 11B (2023) also puts water supply and sewerage in the second phase, while the Ozolnieki “Pūpoliņi” (2024) provides for centralised networks.

This changes the order of work. The internal plumbing can be finished while there is still nothing in the street, if the place of the outlet in the foundations and the place of the inlet in the utility room already match the coming technical conditions. In the city, technical conditions issued by Jelgavas ūdens are valid for 2 years; in the municipality they are issued through the BIS by Jelgavas novada KU.

New-build figures before the first pipe

The meter unit room
not cooler than +5 °C, lit, no further than 2 m from the building’s outer wall (Jelgava regulations No. 19-10, point 13).
Pressure at the inlet
at least 0.26 MPa (2.6 bar) at ground level at peak consumption (point 14); second-floor showers are calculated on it.
Water heater location
in a room that does not fall below 0 °C, with access for changing the anode and more than 60 cm from the bath (Bosch Tronic 2000T).
Frost depth in clay
LBN 003-19 (table 15) gives clay soil in Jelgava a standard frost depth of up to 119 cm for a winter expected once in 100 years; in sandy soil it is more. The depth of the inlet is set by the design.
Checks before handover
for a water main a hydraulic test and a disinfection certificate, for gravity sewerage a CCTV inspection report (point 12).
Consumption norm without a meter
215 l a day per resident in a detached house with a bath or shower (Annex 1).

Connecting new-build water and sewerage

The first connection of a new house is not just tightening a nut. Point 12 of Regulations No. 19-10 requires new or rebuilt systems to be tested before handover: for a water main a hydraulic test and disinfection with a certificate, for gravity sewerage a CCTV inspection of the pipeline. The service pipe in the yard and acceptance are described in sewer and water connection for a private house.

Internal water pipe installation: nothing before the meter

Sub-point 15.8 of Regulations No. 19-10 prohibits making internal water pipe connections before the commercial metering unit. So the first branch, a watering tap too, goes only after the meter. For garden water that does not end up in the sewer, Jelgavas ūdens installs an additional meter for its subscribers, and the company’s authorised staff seal it.

Internal sewerage installation and rain water

Since 2006 Jelgava’s combined sewerage has been converted into a separate system: household waste water apart from rain water. Sub-point 15.7 of the regulations prohibits discharging precipitation and groundwater into household sewerage at will. In a new build the roof downpipes and drainage are therefore not joined to the house outlet, and they need a separate route already in the design.

A water inlet in a house where groundwater is high

In Jelgava Municipality regulations No. 2 (sub-point 21.2) allow the meter unit to be built outside the ownership boundary in an agreed place if raised groundwater at the boundary could damage the instrument or make it hard to read. In Ozolnieki the builder of the networks wrote that groundwater had to be lowered continuously during the works. The place and depth of the inlet are therefore agreed before the foundations are poured.

Questions people ask

Can a meter unit be placed in an unheated basement?

Only if the air there in winter is not cooler than +5 °C. That is what point 13 of Jelgava regulations No. 19-10 requires, and the place must also be lit and no further than 2 m from the outer wall.

May a watering tap be connected before the meter?

No. Sub-point 15.8 of Regulations No. 19-10 prohibits any internal water pipe connections before the commercial metering unit. For watering, Jelgavas ūdens offers an additional meter.

What pressure at the inlet can be expected in Jelgava?

Regulations No. 19-10 (point 14) set a minimum of 0.26 MPa, or 2.6 bar, at ground level at peak consumption. The actual pressure on a particular street is measured with a pressure gauge at the inlet.
