What is the water hardness in Jelgava and what does it mean for a water heater?

Water hardness in Jelgava is not stated in the monthly test reports, so the exact figure can be learned from Jelgavas ūdens, while a test strip or the limescale in the water heater itself shows it roughly.

The reports give other indicators: electrical conductivity, iron and sulphates. For a water heater this means: how often it needs servicing is set by what you see in the tank and on the heating element.

Water from the Tetele artesian wells

Jelgavas ūdens states that only groundwater is used for drinking water and the centralised water supply in the city. Since January 2014 the city has been supplied by the Tetele well field: 9 new and 3 reconstructed artesian wells able to give 20,736 m³ per day, with a supply pipeline in 2 lines.

Since May 2014 the water has been filtered to remove iron, and sulphates are reduced by nanofiltration. The water supplied to the city has iron below 0.1 mg/l and sulphates within 200–250 mg/l.

The city’s water supply has a long history: from 1660 to 1882 Jelgava used water from the river Svēte, and in 1975 the Tetele pumping station with 10 artesian wells was handed over. The composition of the groundwater is what determines the amount of limescale in a water heater.

What Jelgavas ūdens tests show and what they do not

In a BIOR test on 15 September 2026, a sample from a cold-water tap at Pārlielupe Primary School: electrical conductivity 742 µS/cm, pH 7.4, iron below 20 µg/l (norm 200 µg/l), sulphates 221 mg/l (norm 250 mg/l). On 20 May 2025, at Filozofu iela 50 and Meiju ceļš 31, pH was 7.6 and 7.7, and conductivity 774 and 783 µS/cm.

Conductivity shows the total of dissolved salts, not calcium and magnesium directly, so hardness cannot be read from it. The monthly reports contain microbiology, turbidity, conductivity, colour, pH, iron and sulphates, but not hardness. Latvian law does not have hardness grades either; for example, section 9 of the German WRMG counts water as soft below 1.5 mmol/l and hard above 2.5 mmol/l.

Sources: Jelgavas ūdens sections “Water supply system” and “Water analyses”, BIOR test reports No. DC-2026-P-59621.01 and PV-2025-P-30620.01, 30621.01; the German WRMG § 9 is only an example, not a Latvian norm; checked on 19 November 2026.

How to find out the hardness at your tap

Limescale in the water heater
Bosch (Tronic 2000T) advises that if limescale builds up in the water heater or the safety group and the cause is raised hardness, you should consider more frequent servicing or water treatment.

The water heater's year: from fitting to anode swap

  1. Before fitting. Compare the water with the manufacturer’s requirements. The Bosch Tronic 2000T requires a hardness of at least 120 ppm (7.2 °dH), pH 6.5–9.5 and conductivity 130–1,500 µS/cm; the 742–783 µS/cm measured in Jelgava tests lies within that range.
  2. In the first year. Bosch requires the magnesium anode to be checked every year and replaced if necessary; operating the water heater without the anode is prohibited.
  3. When limescale appears. The manufacturer’s service list includes cleaning the inside of the tank, removing limescale from the heating element or replacing it, checking the anode and replacing the flange seal.
  4. Limescale in the safety group. In Bosch’s fault table this is a separate item: the limescale is removed and, if necessary, the safety group is replaced.

Water quality or water heater servicing: whom to ask

For the water in the street network, its composition and the hardness figure, ask Jelgavas ūdens; in Ozolnieki and other villages of the municipality, ask Jelgavas novada KU, whose villages have their own water supply systems. Limescale in a water heater, the anode and the heating element are a plumber’s job. Questions people ask

Does a conductivity of 742 µS/cm mean the water in Jelgava is hard?

No, that cannot be concluded from this figure. Electrical conductivity shows all dissolved salts, including sulphates, of which there are 188–221 mg/l in Jelgava’s water. Ask Jelgavas ūdens about hardness or measure it with a test strip.

Is the water in Ozolnieki the same as in Jelgava?

No, it is a different system. In the water test reports of Jelgavas novada KU, Ozolnieki Parish has its own Ozolnieki water supply system, Āne and Tetele share one, and Brankas has its own.

Is water that is too soft a problem for a water heater too?

For some manufacturers, yes. For example, the Bosch Tronic 2000T manual states a minimum hardness of 120 ppm, or 7.2 °dH, so before installing a softener check your water heater’s requirements.
