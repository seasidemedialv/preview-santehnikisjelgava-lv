What changes in a flat's plumbing when the building is renovated

When a block of flats in Jelgava is renovated, three points of the rules touch the flat’s plumbing: building waste must not enter the sewer, rainwater has to be led away separately from household wastewater, and rebuilt systems are tested before handover.

Buildings put up after 1944

In the fighting of the Second World War in 1944, almost 90% of Jelgava was destroyed, and the city was rebuilt in the Soviet style. In 2010, LV portāls wrote that Jelgavas nekustamā īpašuma pārvalde, as the largest manager, was then looking after 435 residential buildings with about 14,500 flats.

Before the decision, during the works, after handover

  1. Before the owners' decision. In Jelgava the water services contract for a block of flats with Jelgavas ūdens is concluded by the flat owners, who authorise one person by a decision of the owners (regulations No. 19-10, points 39.2 and 40.2). The community of flat owners decides on rebuilding the building’s networks; in a particular building the documents may say otherwise.
  2. During the works. Points 34.2 and 34.3 of the regulations prohibit discharging into the sewer substances that clog networks: lime, sand, plaster, soil, textiles, and metal and wood shavings. Mortar or plaster water in a sink or toilet is exactly that.
  3. Before handover. New or rebuilt systems are tested before handover: a hydraulic test and a disinfection certificate for the water pipe, a CCTV inspection report for a gravity sewer, a hydraulic test certificate for a pressure main (point 12).
  4. After handover, in the flat. The flat’s connections to the new risers, the traps and the internal pipes are re-laid so that they match the new outlets; what is part of the building’s works is set by the project and the owners' decision.

Rainwater away from the household sewer

Since the new treatment plant was handed over in 2006, Jelgava’s combined sewer has been converted into a separate one: household wastewater apart from rainwater. Point 53 of regulations No. 19-10 set that a user whose rainwater goes into the household sewer must, within five years of 2 August 2019, rebuild the outlet and build a separate connection to the city’s storm sewer; that deadline ended in August 2024.

In a renovation, when the roof and downpipes are changed, it is a natural moment to finish this separation. Jelgavas ūdens tests its networks with a smoke generator, looking for places where rainwater or drainage water enters the household sewer.

The rules that apply to a building being renovated

In the city, Jelgava City binding regulations No. 19-10 are in force. Under point 20, the owner is responsible for operating the water supply and sewer systems of their property and stops leaks in them at their own expense. In Jelgava Municipality, for example in Ozolnieki or Kalnciems, municipality regulations No. 2 are in force: where the outlets of several houses are connected in series, the responsibility boundary is the first chamber of the first house (point 20.2).

If after a soil stack renewal a drip appears at a new joint, point 21 of the regulations requires the owner to report leaks in their own water supply or sewer system to the water service provider without delay. If the building has no meters yet, annex 1 assumes 150 litres per day per resident for a block of flats.

Points of city regulations No. 19-10 on this page: 12, 20, 21, 34, 39, 40 and 53; in the municipality, point 20.2 of regulations No. 2. Texts checked on 19 November 2026. A particular building’s documents may say otherwise.

The building manager's job and the plumber's job

Replacing a building’s soil stacks and risers, the basement main and the separation of rainwater is usually organised by the building manager or the owners' authorised person, and the service pipe to the street network by Jelgavas ūdens. In the flat, after a renovation, the plumber re-lays the connections up to the new soil stack. Renewing a drain branch in a flat costs from €30 per outlet, a water riser section for one storey from €110, a soil stack section Ø110 from €100. On this: riser renewal.

Questions people ask

May plaster or mortar water be poured down a sink during a renovation?

Not in Jelgava. Point 34.2 of regulations No. 19-10 prohibits discharging lime, sand, plaster and soil into the sewer. At the treatment plant, screens with 3 mm gaps catch sand and rags, but the building’s pipes and soil stacks clog before that.

Why does a renovated building have to lead rainwater away separately?

Jelgava’s sewer has been converted to a separate system since 2006, and point 53 of regulations No. 19-10 required the outlets of rainwater to be rebuilt within five years of 2 August 2019.

Who concludes the water contract for a block of flats in Jelgava?

The flat owners authorise one person by a decision of the owners, and the management agreement or the owners' decision is attached to the contract (regulations No. 19-10, points 39.2 and 40.2).
