Basement flooding in spring floods: what to do before and after

If a basement floods in spring floods, most of the work can be done before the flood water arrives: checking the pump, the backflow valve and the discharge pipe.

When the water comes, it is pumped to a place where it may be discharged, not into the household sewer. Once the water has receded, the valves and the pump are inspected before they are needed again.

Why water gets into Jelgava basements

Jelgava lies on a plain, only about 3.5 m above mean sea level, and in high water both the plain and the city itself can flood. On 15 April 2013 the Lielupe at Jelgava stood at 213 cm against a flood limit of 260 cm, yet several streets were already under water, mainly because of high water in the tributaries. Because of the flat terrain, the city’s sewer system needs 32 pumping stations.

Water comes not only in spring. In the July 2024 downpour, 11 multi-storey buildings in Jelgava stood in water, along with the yards and basements of many private houses; pumping in many places did not help because there was nowhere to pump to. On 30 July 2024, LSM reported that 180 mm, or 203% of the July norm, was recorded in Kalnciems Parish.

In the municipality, the head of the council named the most vulnerable places in March 2026: the Lielupe on the Kalnciems and Staļģene side, the allotments on the Jelgava side that have been converted into residential zones, and Ūziņi by the Svēte. The Svēte can burst its banks within three hours, so the pump is checked before a warning, not after it.

From February to July: what to do and when

  1. In February, before the floods. Run the drainage pump for one cycle with water in the pit. Wilo (Drain TMW 32) states that if the pump does not switch on, first check that the float switch moves freely.
  2. Before March. Check that the discharge pipe is protected from frost and has a gate valve and a backflow valve, as Wilo requires for its pump. In 2026, Bāra ceļš was flooded as early as 12 March.
  3. When the water rises. Compare the inflow with the pump’s capacity: the Wilo TMW 32/8 pumps up to 10 m³/h, the TMW 32/11 up to 14.5 m³/h. If the inflow is greater, the pit will overflow, and a more powerful or a second pump is needed.
  4. While the pump is running. Do not let it run in a dry pit, because dry running can damage the seal. Discharge the water where the building documents or an agreement provide, not into the household sewer.
  5. After the water recedes. If the pump runs but does not pump, Wilo names a clogged check valve as the cause, which is cleaned or replaced. Open and clean the backflow valve in the sewer outlet too.
  6. In summer, during long idle time. Run one cycle every 2 months, and after 1,500 operating hours the pump needs a service check.

Jelgava's rules on backflow valves and groundwater

Point 23 of Jelgava City binding regulations No. 19-10: if a property lies in the city’s flood-prone area and is connected to the centralised sewer, a backflow valve must be built into its outlet. The boundary of the flood-prone area is set by the local authority; in the LVĢMC flood risk maps, which are a different document, the places at risk from spring floods are the historic centre, Jelgava Palace with its park, and the Ģintermuiža development.

Point 15.7 of the same document prohibits discharging precipitation water and groundwater into the centralised sewer at will. A drainage pump hose in a household sewer manhole is therefore not a solution. If drainage is connected to an outlet without building documents, Jelgavas ūdens may calculate compensation by the inner diameter of the pipe.

Jelgava City binding regulations No. 19-10 (version from 1 July 2020), points 15.7. and 23; Jelgavas ūdens warning about compensation; checked on 19 November 2026. In Jelgava Municipality, municipality binding regulations No. 2 are in force.

Who does which job: Jelgavas ūdens, the building manager or a plumber

If after spring floods the sewer is blocked and it is unclear where the fault lies, Jelgavas ūdens, when its office is closed, first inspects the site and clarifies the responsibility boundary of the networks; the company provides network repair to its own customers. The basement of a block of flats and its pumps are a matter for the building manager.

In a private house, fitting a pump, building a backflow valve into the outlet and re-routing a discharge pipe is a plumber’s job. The price of fitting a basement or drainage pump depends on the amount of work, access and materials; an urgent call-out costs from €60. Questions people ask

May a drainage pump be discharged into the household sewer if there is water in the basement?

In Jelgava, not without approval. Point 15.7 of regulations No. 19-10 prohibits discharging groundwater and precipitation water into the sewer at will, and for unapproved drainage Jelgavas ūdens may calculate compensation.

Why is the pump running but the water level in the basement is not falling?

One cause is an inflow greater than the pump’s capacity: for example, the Wilo TMW 32/8 pumps up to 10 m³/h. The other is a clogged check valve, which Wilo advises cleaning or replacing.

Does Jelgava city's backflow valve rule apply in Kalnciems?

Regulations No. 19-10 apply to the city of Jelgava; in Kalnciems, Jelgava Municipality regulations No. 2 are in force. By LVĢMC data Kalnciems begins to flood when the Lielupe reaches 1.96 m LAS, so a backflow valve in the outlet is worth considering there even without the city’s requirement.
