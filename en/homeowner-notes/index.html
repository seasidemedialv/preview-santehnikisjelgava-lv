Three articles and the fact each one starts from

Jelgava lies on a plain only 3.5 m above mean sea level. In LVĢMC’s flood risk plan, flooding of the Lielupe flood plain at Jelgava begins at a water level of 1.55 m LAS, and flooding of the city’s lowest areas at 2.65 m LAS. In March 2026 the head of the municipal council named the most vulnerable places as the Lielupe on the Kalnciems and Staļģene side and the Jelgava side where allotments have been turned into residential zones. What to do before and after the water: A basement floods in spring floods.

Since 2014 Jelgava has been supplied by the Tetele well field with 9 new and 3 rebuilt artesian wells. Jelgavas ūdens treats the water with filtration to remove iron and with nanofiltration to reduce sulphates. The company publishes test reports every month, but hardness is not given in them. How to find it out: Water hardness in Jelgava and limescale in a water heater.

Since 2006 Jelgava’s combined sewerage has been converted to a separate one, so that household wastewater flows apart from rainwater. Regulations No. 19-10 gave users whose rainwater reached the household sewer five years from 2 August 2019 to rebuild the outlet. What this means for a flat: What changes in a flat’s plumbing when the building is renovated.
