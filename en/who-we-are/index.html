City and municipality: two water companies

In the city of Jelgava, water and sewerage are run by Jelgavas ūdens, and in the municipality’s villages by Jelgavas novada KU. The city is governed by Jelgava City binding regulations No. 19-10 and the municipality by Jelgava Municipality binding regulations No. 2, and each has its own responsibility boundaries and connection rules. The municipality’s villages differ: Kārniņi and Mežciems take their water from a borehole (LVĢMC, 2014), and the environmental report of the municipality’s territory plan lists separate treatment plants for the two villages.

We do the plumbing of homes and flats in both places: from a tap and trap to the riser, the water heater and the yard line. For connection, meter sealing or street network questions, contact Jelgavas ūdens in the city and Jelgavas novada KU in the municipality. Jobs by group are on the page Plumbing services in Jelgava.

21 districts and 10 places in the municipality

In the city we work in 21 districts on both banks of the Lielupe, from Lapskalns in the north to Dienvidu gals and Viskaļi in the south. In Pārlielupe these are the RAF district, Cukurfabrika, Ozolpils, Langervalde and Cepļi, and between the Driksa and the Lielupe the district of Salas.

In the municipality we work in Ozolnieki, in the villages of Cenu parish (Āne, Tetele, Brankas, Dalbe, Jaunpēternieki), in Kalnciems and in Jaunsvirlauka parish (Mežciems, Kārniņi, Jaunsvirlauka). The form’s list has 31 places in total, and for a place outside it there is the choice “Other”. Each place has its own page in the section Where we work.
