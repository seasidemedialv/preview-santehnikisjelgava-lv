A machine in the basement: household sewerage, not a drainage pit

Sub-point 15.7 of Jelgava City binding regulations No. 19-10 prohibits arbitrarily discharging precipitation and groundwater into centralised sewerage. If the basement already has a drainage pit with a pump, the machine’s outlet is connected to household sewerage, but the drainage is not joined to it.

Jelgavas ūdens checks its networks with a smoke generator, looking for places where rain, ground or drainage water enters household sewerage, and for drainage connected to an outlet without construction documents it can calculate compensation by the pipe’s internal diameter. So when a machine is connected in a basement, it is worth making sure where the outlet really goes.

Sieramuiža between the Platone and the Vircava

Sieramuiža is in south-east Jelgava, and it is separated from the rest of the city by the Platone, which runs fast during floods; the Vircava also joins the Lielupe near Sieramuiža. The district comprises 31 streets, and the fields of the former manor now hold allotments. The Lielupe basin plan, outside the nationally significant territory, includes restoring the Platone over a length of 21.66 km.

Water can come into a basement in two different ways. In the downpour on the evening of 28 July 2024, the rain pumping stations could not drain the large volume of water, and in private-house areas the rain flooded yards; LSM reported on 30 July 2024 that 11 multi-storey blocks and the basements of many houses were under water. That is surface water. Backflow from the sewer is a different route, and against it, in the city’s flood-prone area, point 23 of the regulations requires a backflow valve in the house outlet.

For a machine in a basement this means: it is put on a raised base, not straight on the floor, and the outlet is run so that it is not the lowest point where water from the yard enters first.

The outlet can be joined to the sink trap’s branch if the machine stands next to the sink, or to a separate wall trap in the laundry nook. If there is no outlet in the wall, it is a new point from €35.

A fridge water line after the meter

Sub-point 15.8 of Regulations No. 19-10 prohibits connections before the meter, so the dishwasher tap is branched off the cold water line under the sink, after the meter, and placed next to the cabinet so that it can be closed without pulling out the machine. The drain hose is joined to the sink trap’s branch; if the sink is still to come, see kitchen sink fitting.

A fridge line is branched off the cold water after the meter, a small shut-off valve is put in an accessible place and a thin pipe is taken to the appliance without sharp bends. The fridge is pushed back with a loop in the pipe, so that it can be pulled out for cleaning.

Questions people ask

Why is there water by the washing machine in the basement after a downpour?

There are two routes: rain water from the yard, or backflow from the sewer through the outlet. A backflow valve does not help against the first, but it does against the second: in the city’s flood-prone area, point 23 of Regulations No. 19-10 requires it in the house outlet; ask the local government for the area’s boundary.

Why does a machine on an upper floor fill slowly?

Regulations No. 19-10 provide for at least 0.26 MPa at the inlet at ground level, and higher up the pressure is lower. More often, though, the fault is a clogged strainer at the end of the inlet hose or a partly closed tap.

Does a built-in dishwasher need a separate tap?

Yes, a separate shut-off tap lets you close only the machine while the sink tap works. It is placed next to the cabinet so that it can be reached without pulling out the machine.
