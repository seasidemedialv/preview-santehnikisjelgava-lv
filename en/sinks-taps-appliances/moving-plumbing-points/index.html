New plumbing points in Jelgava - water points and outlets in a new place

New plumbing points in Jelgava are work done before the finishes: water supply pipes and drain outlets are run from the riser or the building’s inlet to where the basin, toilet, shower or machine will stand.

Each finished position is charged as a point, from €35 per outlet, usually €35–80 per outlet.

Meter unit: +5 °C, within 2 m of the outer wall

Point 13 of Jelgava City binding regulations No. 19-10 allows the commercial metering unit on the inlet to be built in a room where the air temperature is not lower than +5 °C. The place must be easy to reach, lit and no further than 2 m from the building’s outer wall. Sub-point 15.8 prohibits connections before this unit, while sub-point 15.9 requires local and centralised water supply to be separated by an air gap.

In a private house this means every new water point starts after the meter, and when rooms are rebuilt, the meter’s place is not closed up with the finishes. If the house also has its own well, its pipes are not connected to the city water pipe.

In Jelgava Municipality the procedure is different. Municipality regulations No. 2 (sub-point 21.2) allow the meter unit to be built outside the ownership boundary in an agreed place if raised groundwater could damage the instrument. Jelgavas novada KU states that in a detached house without a commercial meter consumption is calculated at 15 m³ a month for each declared resident, while a flat meter’s verification period is 4 years, and it is sealed by the company’s specialists or in their presence.

Source on the meter unit and branches: Jelgava City binding regulations No. 19-10, likumi.lv; checked 19 November 2026.

New points: limits from the regulations

Temperature at the meter
in the city not lower than +5 °C (Regulations No. 19-10, point 13). Pipes in cold rooms are insulated.
Meter distance from the outer wall
no more than 2 m (point 13). New branches start only after it.
Pressure at the inlet
at least 0.26 MPa (point 14). A long, thin new service pipe to the furthest point lowers this pressure.
Drain slope
20 ‰ for a toilet’s 75–100 mm pipe, 30 ‰ for a thinner one up to 50 mm (LBN 221-15). Every metre of distance needs height under the floor.
Flat meter in the municipality
verification every 4 years, sealed by Jelgavas novada KU (the company’s explanation).
Checks before handover
for a new or rebuilt service pipe to the street network, a hydraulic test and disinfection certificate, and for gravity sewerage a CCTV inspection report (Regulations No. 19-10, point 12).

Moving and rebuilding plumbing outlets

Point 20 of Regulations No. 19-10 says that the owner is responsible for the water supply and sewerage system of their property and fixes leaks in it at their own expense. Moved points in a wall and floor are part of this system, and later they can no longer be inspected.

So before the finishes the new supply pipes are tested under pressure and the drains with water, while the pipes are still visible. As few joints as possible are left in the wall, and at the end of each point a shut-off valve or plug is left until the appliance is fitted.

Moving a toilet: a slope or a pump behind the toilet

In flats in blocks the toilet usually stands next to the soil stack. For a toilet outlet without calculation, LBN 221-15 provides a slope of 2 cm per metre, so the distance it can be moved is limited by how high the floor or false wall can be raised.

If the slope cannot be achieved, the solution can be a factory-made compact pump unit directly behind the toilet; where it may be placed is determined by the unit’s manufacturer and the building code. The toilet itself is fitted after the outlet is built; that is described on the page Toilet fitting.

Example: moving a kitchen in Mežciems

In Mežciems, next to the former MRS complex, there are two 24-flat three-storey houses. Suppose that in a flat the kitchen is moved to the room side and needs a cold and hot water point and an outlet for the sink there, as well as a branch for the dishwasher. That is four points, 4 × from €35, at least €140 in total. Mežciems is in Jelgava Municipality, so the municipality regulations No. 2 apply there, not the city regulations No. 19-10. Questions people ask

Can a new water point be branched off before the flat's meter?

In the city, no: Regulations No. 19-10 (sub-point 15.8) prohibit internal water pipe connections before the commercial metering unit. In the municipality the procedure is set by Jelgava Municipality regulations No. 2; there too, new points are usually branched off after the meter.

May a small pump unit be placed behind the toilet?

Usually yes: a compact factory pump unit can be placed directly behind the toilet if the slope cannot be achieved for the outlet to the riser. The installation requirements are given by the unit’s manufacturer.

How are new supply pipes tested before tiling?

With water pressure while the pipes are still visible; the pressure and duration are set by the pipe manufacturer’s instructions. If the service pipe to the street network is also rebuilt, Regulations No. 19-10 (point 12) require a hydraulic test and disinfection certificate.
