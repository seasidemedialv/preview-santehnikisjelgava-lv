A basement sink in a flood-prone area

Point 23 of Jelgava City binding regulations No. 19-10: if a property is in the flood-prone area of Jelgava city and is connected to centralised sewerage, a backflow valve must be built into its drain outlet. This area is set by the local government’s plan; the LVĢMC flood risk map, which shows the historic centre and the Ģintermuiža buildings as at risk from spring floods, is a different document.

A trap does not protect against backflow: it holds only a few centimetres of water. If there is a sink or floor drain trap in the basement and the street sewer fills during floods, water can come up through the trap. So when a trap is swapped in a basement, it is worth checking whether the house outlet has a backflow valve; there is more on the page Water in the basement.

Swapping a basin trap

Sub-points 34.2 and 34.3 of Regulations No. 19-10 prohibit putting substances that clog the networks into sewers: solid objects, textiles, sand, oils, fats and unground food waste. That is exactly what stays in a sink trap, so the inside of the old trap shows what is behind a slow drain.

The new trap is joined to the outlet with a rubber cuff or seal that matches the outlet diameter. After fitting, the basin is filled to the brim and let out: if it stays dry under the nut, the joint is fine.

Swapping a bath trap

Point 20 of Regulations No. 19-10 requires the owner to fix leaks in their own system. A leak from a bath trap is often not visible in the bathroom itself; the neighbour below is the first to notice it.

To swap a bath trap you must reach the side of the bath; if it is tiled, a hatch is needed. When swapping a bath trap, it is worth checking the adapter to the outlet too, especially if it is still cast iron.

Questions people ask

What should be done with a basement floor drain trap after floods?

After the water recedes, sand and mud can remain in the floor drain trap, so it is opened and cleaned and the water seal is checked to see it stands again. If the house has a backflow valve in the outlet, that is opened and cleaned too, so that the flap does not hold back the house’s waste water.

Does a trap protect a basement from sewage backflow in floods?

No. In a flood-prone area Regulations No. 19-10 (point 23) require a backflow valve in the sewer outlet. The trap there is only a water seal against smell.

Can a plastic trap be connected to an old cast iron pipe?

It can, with a rubber adapter that presses into the cast iron socket. If the end of the cast iron pipe has cracked or the socket has rusted, that section is replaced first.
