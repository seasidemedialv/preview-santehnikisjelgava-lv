Water at a Jelgava tap: filtered groundwater

In the city, Jelgavas ūdens filters the water before supply to remove iron, and nanofiltration reduces sulphate to 200–250 mg/l. Hardness is not published in the monthly reports; if white deposits build up in a cartridge or aerator, you can ask the company itself for the figure.

In Jelgava Municipality, Jelgavas novada KU states that the naturally raised sulphate content of the groundwater can contribute to corrosion of metal water pipes, including a building’s internal ones. There, rusty particles in a tap are a reason to look at the metal supply pipes after the meter too.

A tap on village water: Kalnciems, Mežciems, Kārniņi

In the municipality’s villages, water comes from separate systems. In 2016 the Kalnciems system supplied 129 m³ of water a day to about 2,000 consumers, and the water is treated before supply, while in Mežciems and Kārniņi, according to LVĢMC data for 2014, water from a borehole was supplied untreated.

What water is like in your village can be seen in the JNKU test reports. If deposits in the aerator or the cartridge strainer keep coming back, they are flushed out, and for a private house a local filter to reduce sulphate or hardness can be considered, which the company itself mentions. A filter and its connection are a separate job, not a tap repair.

Swapping a mixer tap cartridge and the supply hoses

The cartridge is unscrewed after both angle valves under the basin are closed, and the new one is chosen by the tap’s manufacturer, model and the size of the removed cartridge. If the cartridge is intact but the seat in the body is scratched, the new cartridge will drip too, and then the tap is replaced. If the angle valve under the basin no longer closes, the water to the tap is shut off at the flat’s valve by the meter, and swapping that valve is a separate item within the same hourly work.

A supply hose is replaced if its braid has rusted, it has swollen or the joint at the angle valve is damp. In the city, point 20 of Regulations No. 19-10 makes the owner fix leaks in their own system at their own expense, and the hose under the basin is a place where a leak starts quietly.

Example: two taps in a house in Miezīte

The main buildings in Miezīte are family houses. Suppose the kitchen and bathroom taps in a house drip, and cartridges for both models can still be bought. If swapping both cartridges takes 2 hours, at the hourly rate from €25/h that is at least €50. If the body of one tap turns out to be damaged and it is replaced, a mixer tap swap from €30 is added. Questions people ask

How do I find out which cartridge my tap needs?

By the tap’s manufacturer and model, if known, or by the size of the removed cartridge and the number of splines on the stem. The surest way is to take out the old cartridge and bring it with you to the shop.

Do metal tap connections rust faster in the municipality?

Jelgavas novada KU states that the naturally raised sulphate content of the municipality’s groundwater can contribute to corrosion of metal water pipes, including a building’s internal ones, and advises keeping an eye on the condition of the pipes. A rusted angle valve is worth replacing in the same visit as the tap.

Does Jelgava water need a filter before the mixer tap?

In the BIOR test on 15 September 2026 the iron in the tap water was below 20 µg/l, so a filter is usually fitted because of a building’s pipes, not because of the network water. For hardness, ask Jelgavas ūdens.
