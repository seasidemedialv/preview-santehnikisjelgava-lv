Water point and outlet: two connections for every appliance

Almost every appliance in this section needs two connections: a water point with a shut-off valve and a drain outlet with a trap. A basin and a shower have two water points (cold and hot), a washing machine has one. If the points are already in place, the job is only fitting the appliance.

If there is no point, it is built separately: one water or drain connection point costs from €35, usually €35–80. On the call, say whether the supply pipes are already in the wall and, if you can, photograph them. Then on the call it can be decided whether only the fitting is needed or the points as well.

Water from Tetele: 742 µS/cm and 221 mg/l of sulphate at the tap

Since 2014 the city of Jelgava has been supplied by the Tetele well field. The water is treated to remove iron, and nanofiltration reduces the sulphate (Jelgavas ūdens). In a BIOR test commissioned by Jelgavas ūdens, a sample from a cold water tap at Pārlielupe Primary School on 15 September 2026 showed conductivity of 742 µS/cm, iron below 20 µg/l, sulphate 221 mg/l and a pH of 7.4 in the sample.

For taps this means that brown deposits in an aerator or cartridge usually come from the building’s old pipes rather than from the city water, judging by this BIOR test. Jelgavas ūdens does not give hardness in its monthly reports, so if white deposits build up in the taps, you can ask the company for the exact figure.

Pressure has a limit of its own: point 14 of Jelgava City binding regulations No. 19-10 requires at least 0.26 MPa (2.6 bar) at the building’s inlet at ground level at peak consumption. On the upper floors and at the end of a long service pipe the pressure at the tap is lower, which thermostatic mixer taps notice, and machines fill more slowly.

Jelgavas ūdens water supply description and the September 2026 test report; Regulations No. 19-10, point 14. Checked 19 November 2026.

Water connections only after the meter

Sub-point 15.8 of Regulations No. 19-10 prohibits internal water pipe connections before the commercial metering unit, while sub-point 15.9 does not allow local and centralised water supply to be combined; they must be separated by an air gap. So a new tap, machine or fridge line is branched off after the meter.

In the villages of Jelgava Municipality, for example Ozolnieki, Āne or Kalnciems, water is supplied by Jelgavas novada KU, and Jelgava Municipality regulations No. 2 apply there. Fitting appliances is the same, but check the relevant points in the municipality’s regulations or ask the company.

A leak under the sink: points 20 and 21 of Regulations No. 19-10

Point 20 of Regulations No. 19-10 says that the owner or holder is responsible for the water supply and sewerage system of their property. They fix leaks in it at their own expense. Point 21 requires leaks in their own system to be reported to the service provider without delay.

Angle valves, supply hoses, traps and machine taps are part of this system. So when one appliance is changed, it is worth looking at the neighbouring parts too. A rusted valve or a swollen hose is cheaper to replace in the same visit than after a drip in the cupboard. If water is already running, first close the inlet valve and then fill in the form.

Example: a flat in Lapskalns, two taps and a washing machine

Lapskalns is a large district in the north of Jelgava with blocks of flats, detached houses and allotments. Suppose that in a flat in a block you need to swap the mixer tap in the kitchen and in the bathroom and connect a new washing machine to the existing tap and outlet. Two taps: 2 × from €30, washing machine hook-up from €35, at least €95 in total. Labour price, materials extra; the plumber tells you about the trip on the call before the job starts.

Questions people ask

What water pressure is required at a building's inlet in Jelgava?

Regulations No. 19-10 (point 14) require at least 0.26 MPa, or 2.6 bar, at the building’s inlet at ground level at peak consumption. At a tap on an upper floor the pressure is lower.

Are the water rules in Ozolnieki and Kalnciems the same as in the city?

No. In the city, Jelgava City binding regulations No. 19-10 apply; in the municipality, Jelgava Municipality regulations No. 2 (2025). Fitting appliances is the same, but each set of rules defines its own responsibility boundaries and procedures.

Why are Jelgavas ūdens reports silent on water hardness?

In its monthly reports Jelgavas ūdens publishes microbiology, turbidity, conductivity, colour, pH, iron and sulphate, but not hardness. You can ask the company for the exact figure.
