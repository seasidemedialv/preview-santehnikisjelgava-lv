Ozolnieki and Kalnciems: sulphate and metal connections

In the villages of Jelgava Municipality, water is supplied by Jelgavas novada KU, and its water testing reports list separate systems, for example Ozolnieki, Brankas and Āne, and Tetele. The company states that groundwater across the municipality has a naturally raised sulphate content, which can contribute to corrosion of metal water pipes, including a building’s internal ones, and advises paying attention to the condition of the pipes.

Swapping a basin is the moment to do this: under the basin there are angle valves, hose nuts and sometimes an old metal branch from the wall. If any of them has rusted, it is replaced together with the basin, because afterwards it has to be reached through the new vanity unit. In the municipality, Jelgava Municipality binding regulations No. 2 apply, not the city regulations.

Sources: Jelgavas novada KU on water quality; Jelgava Municipality binding regulations No. 2. Checked 19 November 2026.

Meiju ceļš 14, 16 and 20: a basin in a 464-series block

Jelgava has four 464-series blocks of flats, and three of them are on Meiju ceļš, Nos. 14, 16 and 20. The surveyed buildings of this series were commissioned in 1964–1978 and have five storeys above ground and one below. In total, more than ten blocks of flats are marked on the map on Meiju ceļš.

In a block like this, the basin’s water usually comes from the riser, and the first point is the flat’s shut-off valves. If they no longer hold, the supply can be closed only at the riser, which is done with the building manager’s knowledge, so arrange the basin swap in good time.

Sources: Ministry of Economics survey of 464-series buildings; OpenStreetMap (Meiju ceļš). Checked 19 November 2026.

Swapping a basin in a bathroom in a timber house

In the fighting of the Second World War in 1944, almost 90% of Jelgava was destroyed, but around Vecpilsētas iela a part of the 18th and 19th century timber architecture has survived (Wikipedia). In a timber house or a frame partition, the basin screws must go into the studs or a mounting board behind the cladding; if there is no board, one is added before the basin is hung.

In a house like this, the water supply pipes to the basin may also run exposed along the wall. If the basin height changes, the pipes are moved, and each moved point is from €35.

Example: a flat on Meiju ceļš 16

Suppose that in a flat in a 464-series block you need to swap the basin and the tap, but the new basin is higher and narrower, so both water supply pipes have to be moved. Basin fitting from €40, tap swap from €30, two moved points 2 × from €35, at least €140 in total. Labour price, materials extra; you go through the trip on the same call before the job.

Questions people ask

Can a basin in a 464-series block be moved to another wall?

It can, if the new outlet reaches the riser with the slope needed and the water supply pipes can be taken behind the meter. A longer move is described on the page moving plumbing points.

Are metal connections suitable for basin supply pipes in Ozolnieki?

They are, but Jelgavas novada KU states that the naturally raised sulphate content of the municipality’s water can contribute to corrosion of metal pipes, and advises keeping an eye on their condition. A rusted valve or nut is cheaper to replace together with the basin.

Is there sulphate in the city's water too?

Yes, but less: Jelgavas ūdens reduces it by nanofiltration to 200–250 mg/l, and in a BIOR test on 15 September 2026 the sulphate content at the tap of Pārlielupe Primary School was 221 mg/l, against a norm of 250 mg/l. It is worth looking at the metal connections under the basin in the city too when the basin is swapped.
