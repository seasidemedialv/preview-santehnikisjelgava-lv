Catering on a building's ground floor: point 24

Point 24 of Jelgava City binding regulations No. 19-10 requires grease traps in properties with public catering, food production or other activity whose waste water contains fat. If a kitchen sink goes into a café, canteen or a room where food is prepared for sale, the place for the grease trap is planned before the sink is fitted, because it takes room under the sink or in the next room.

The reason is the network behind the house. In this city on flat land, sewerage is kept running by 32 automatic pumping stations, and at the treatment works screens with 3 mm gaps separate rags, sand and fat (Jelgavas ūdens). In the municipality, such questions are handled under municipality regulations No. 2 with Jelgavas novada KU.

Sources: Regulations No. 19-10, point 24; Jelgavas ūdens descriptions of the sewer network and the treatment works. Checked 19 November 2026.

Swapping a kitchen sink in a five-storey block in Pārlielupe

In the 1960s and 1970s multi-storey residential blocks were built in Pārlielupe along Aviācijas, Lāčplēša and P. Lejiņa iela, and on the OpenStreetMap map five-storey blocks are visible at P. Lejiņa iela 3–13. In a flat like this the kitchen outlet goes to the building’s soil stack.

If the kitchen sink is swapped in the same place, the job is a sink, a trap and a tap on the existing supply pipes. Plumbing hook-up in a kitchen in a new place means new points, and the dishwasher branch is planned straight away, after the meter. The soil stack itself is not touched in this job.

Example: a sink by the window on P. Lejiņa iela

Suppose that in the kitchen of a five-storey block the sink is moved 2 m further, to the window: a cold and hot water point and an outlet with a slope of at least 6 cm are needed, then the sink is connected. Three points (3 × from €35) and sink fitting from €40: at least €145.

If a new tap (from €30) is fitted at the same time and a dishwasher is connected (from €35), the labour price is at least €210. Minimum labour price, materials extra.

Questions people ask

Does a kitchen sink in a basement need a backflow valve?

In the city, point 23 of Regulations No. 19-10 requires a backflow valve in the house’s sewer outlet if the property is in the flood-prone area and connected to centralised sewerage. Ask the local government (the building authority) whether your address is in this area.

How many centimetres of slope are needed if the sink is moved 3 m?

By LBN 221-15, at 3 cm per metre for a pipe up to 50 mm, that is at least 9 cm. If there is not that much room under the cabinets, the sink’s place is chosen closer to the riser.

May a dishwasher be connected before the water meter?

No. Regulations No. 19-10 (sub-point 15.8) prohibit internal water pipe connections before the commercial metering unit, so the machine’s branch is made after the meter.
