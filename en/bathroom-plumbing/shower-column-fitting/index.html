Pressure on the upper floors of a Lapskalns block of flats

Lapskalns became a town district fairly recently, when blocks of flats, houses and allotments took shape there. Jelgava regulations No. 19-10 (point 14) set a minimum of 2.6 bar at the inlet at ground level at maximum consumption, not at the shower on an upper floor.

The higher the shower, the lower the pressure at the shower head, and in a block of flats the building’s internal network affects it too. So before choosing a rain head, it helps to measure the pressure at the existing shower mixer in the evening, when the whole building is using water, and compare it with the minimum pressure the manufacturer states. If the measured pressure is below that minimum, a larger head will not increase the flow, and you choose a head for which the manufacturer states a lower minimum pressure. For a thermostatic mixer the manufacturer also states a minimum hot water temperature, and in an electric water heater it must be higher than the thermostat setting.

Ceiling rain shower and a timber wall near Vecpilsētas iela

After the destruction of 1944, part of the 18th and 19th century timber architecture has survived around Vecpilsētas iela.

In a timber or stud wall, the shower rail and the rain head holder are fixed to a support behind the finish, not just to plasterboard or a board. If there is no support there, it is built in before the finish, and that is hourly work from €25/h.

For a rain shower fixed in the ceiling, the supply pipe runs through the ceiling, and the joint needs access through a hatch or a removable panel; this is planned before the ceiling is lined. A wall rail with a rain head needs only the existing mixer outlets, if they are at shower height: that is mixer tap fitting from €30, while for a concealed body each new water point is from €35.

Example: a thermostatic shower system in a Lapskalns flat

Lapskalns has both blocks of flats and houses, so the pressure at the shower is worth measuring in any case. If extra fixing has to be built into the wall for the rail, hourly work from €25/h is added.

Questions people ask

Why does a rain shower run weaker on an upper floor in the evening?

Regulations No. 19-10 (point 14) set at least 2.6 bar at the inlet at ground level at maximum consumption. Each storey up and the evening consumption across the building reduce the pressure at the shower, so the measurement is taken in the evening.

Does the supply for a ceiling rain shower need access?

Yes, the joint at the ceiling holder needs access through a hatch or a removable panel. The supply pipe is taken from after the meter, like any new water point (regulations No. 19-10, point 15.8).

Can a rain shower be fixed to a timber wall in an old Jelgava house?

It can, if the rail and head holder are fixed to a support behind the finish. A heavy rain head is not fixed to a board or plasterboard alone.
