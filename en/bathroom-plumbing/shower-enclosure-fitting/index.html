A shower in the basement: pump, pressure pipe and house outlet

A basement shower is the lowest point in the house. If the shower is below the cover level of the nearest inspection chamber, LBN 221-15 has it connected through a separate outlet with a device against water flowing back from the street network into the basement. If the shower tray is lower than the house outlet, the water is lifted with a pump.

For example, the Wilo (Drain TMW 32) pump is intended for household wastewater without faeces from basins, showers, baths and washing machines; its pressure pipe must be frost-protected, with a gate valve and a backflow valve. A floor drain is not connected to the same pump: in the city, regulations No. 19-10 (point 15.7) forbid discharging groundwater into the central sewer at will.

The house outlet is a separate question. Jelgava regulations No. 19-10 (point 23) require a backflow valve on the sewer outlet for buildings in the city’s flood-prone area, which the municipality’s territorial plan defines. The LVĢMC flood risk map, in which the historic centre and the Ģintermuiža buildings are at risk of spring floods, is a different document, so ask the municipality about a specific plot. More on pumps on the page Basements and pumps.

A drain in the floor or a tray: decide before waterproofing

The drain is built in before the levelling layer of the floor and the waterproofing, and the height of its trap sets how high the floor must be at the drain. The price of fitting the drain depends on the amount of work, access and materials.

When a tray is replaced, the old joint and trap come out together with the tray. If the wall is tiled on plasterboard, the screws of the glass wall profile need a support behind the finish, so it is planned before the wall is lined.

Questions people ask

What pressure does an enclosure with hydromassage nozzles need?

The enclosure manufacturer states it in the data sheet. In Jelgava regulations No. 19-10 set at least 2.6 bar at the inlet at ground level, but at the shower on an upper floor it is less, so the pressure is measured at the existing tap.

Does a basement shower need a backflow valve if the house is not in the flood-prone area?

Regulations No. 19-10 (point 23) require a backflow valve on the house outlet in the city’s flood-prone area. LBN 221-15 also requires a device against basement flooding for fixtures below the level of the nearest chamber cover, elsewhere too. Ask the municipality where the flood-prone area ends (territorial plan).

Can basement shower water be pumped with a drainage pump?

For example, Wilo permits its Drain TMW 32 to pump wastewater without faeces, including from showers, if the liquid temperature is 3–40 °C. It is household wastewater, so the pressure pipe goes into the household sewer, not the stormwater network.
