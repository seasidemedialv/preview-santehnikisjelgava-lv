Mixer fitting costs from €30, each new water or drain point from €35, labour price, materials extra.

New points only after the meter: No. 19-10, point 15.8

Jelgava regulations No. 19-10 (point 15.8) forbid making internal water connections before the commercial metering unit. So bidet supply pipes are taken from existing pipes after the meter, usually from the supply of the nearest toilet or basin.

In a house that has both its own borehole and city water, point 15.9 forbids joining the two systems: they must be separated by an air gap. The bidet is connected to one of them, and no link between the pipes of the two systems is permitted. In the villages of the municipality, Jelgava Municipality binding regulations No. 2 (2025) apply.

Jelgava City binding regulations No. 19-10, points 14, 15.8. and 15.9; Jelgava Municipality binding regulations No. 2 (2025). Checked 19 November 2026.

Bidet connection measurements

Pressure at the inlet: 2.6 bar
Jelgava regulations No. 19-10 (point 14) require at least 0.26 MPa at the inlet at ground level at maximum consumption.
Hot water: 55–70 °C in 20 seconds
LBN 221-15 requires this temperature at the draw-off point; the bidet mixer blends it with a lot of cold water.
Outlet fall: 30 ‰
LBN 221-15 has sections up to 50 mm built without calculation at a fall of 30 ‰; for a bidet outlet of 2 m to the soil stack that is 6 cm of drop.
Water heater: more than 60 cm from the bath
the manufacturer (Bosch, Tronic 2000T) requires the water heater to be placed outside bathroom protection zones 1 and 2, in a room where the temperature does not fall below 0 °C.

Hot water at the bidet: from the existing water heater or a new one

If the bathroom already has hot water for the basin or shower, the hot supply for the bidet is taken from the same line. LBN 221-15 requires hot water to arrive at the draw-off point in 20 seconds, so a very long branch from a distant water heater may not meet this requirement.

If the bidet goes into a room without hot water, for example a separate toilet in a house, the place for a local water heater is chosen by the manufacturer’s requirements. Bosch (Tronic 2000T) states: outside protection zones 1 and 2, more than 60 cm from the bath and so that the anode can be replaced.

Fitting a hygiene shower by the toilet

A hygiene shower is one mixer with a hose by the toilet, fitting from €30. If there are no water points nearby, new points are added at from €35 each (for cold and, if needed, hot water); if there is a basin next to it, a basin mixer with an extra outlet for the hose will do.

For a concealed mixer body the place is left before tiling, while a surface-mounted mixer can go on a finished wall. For the supply of a hygiene shower the same point 15.8 applies: only after the meter.

Example: a bidet in a family house in Miezīte, 2 m from the soil stack

The main buildings in Miezīte are family houses, and in Siena ceļš the water supply and sewer networks were put into operation at the beginning of 2019. Suppose that in a house like this a bidet is put next to the toilet, and the outlet to the soil stack is 2 m long.

Three points (cold, hot water and the outlet) at from €35 each, mixer fitting from €30 and 2 m of outlet pipe at €10/m: in total at least €155 for the work. At a fall of 30 ‰ the outlet needs 6 cm of drop, so its start on the bidet side is raised by that much. The bidet, mixer and pipes extra; on the call you go through the job and the price before the work.

Questions people ask

Can a bidet be connected to the same drain pipe as the toilet?

It can, if the bidet outlet with its own trap joins a line with a branch and the pipe slopes towards the soil stack. A bidet cannot be connected to the toilet cuff or the toilet’s own outlet.

How much fall does a bidet outlet need?

LBN 221-15 has sections up to 50 mm built without calculation at a fall of 30 ‰, which is 3 cm per metre. If the soil stack is 2 m away, the start of the outlet must be 6 cm higher than the joint.

Can the bidet supply be taken from a pipe before the water meter?

Not in Jelgava. Regulations No. 19-10 (point 15.8) forbid connections before the commercial metering unit, so the supply is taken from the pipes after the meter.
