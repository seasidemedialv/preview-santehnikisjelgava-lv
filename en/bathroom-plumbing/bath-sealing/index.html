An outer wall over 197 heating days: the joint that lifts first

For Jelgava, LBN 003-19 uses a heating period of 197 days with an average outdoor air temperature of 0.7 °C, and an average of -2.5 °C in January. In that time the bathroom’s outer wall and the tiles by it are cooler than the room air, and after a shower moisture condenses on them right along the joint line.

Sanitary silicone cures by taking up moisture from the air, but the surface under the joint must be dry, or it lifts. So it is better not to use the bath the day before the work and to air the room, and by an outer wall the surface is dried especially carefully. After laying, the joint is left to cure before use for as long as the particular sealant’s manufacturer states.

Replacing silicone in a bathroom: the joint by the outer wall

Water behind a lifted joint damages the floor and the neighbour’s ceiling. The joint by the outer wall feels it most, where in winter the tile is at its coolest.

By an inner wall the tile is as warm as the room, so the joint there stays dry longer. By an outer wall moisture condenses on the tile all through the heating season, and the joint there turns black faster. So the joint by the outer wall is worth looking at every spring, not only when stains appear.

The work itself can be described in one sentence: the old joint is cut out completely, the surface is cleaned and dried, and only then is the new one laid. The choice of material matters more. For a bath joint, sanitary silicone made for damp rooms is used; acrylic sealant and building silicone do not last long in a zone of constant water. A corner trim does not seal over an old joint: the trim goes on only over a new, dry joint.

Questions people ask

Can a bath joint be replaced in winter?

It can. A dry surface matters more than the month: the bath is not used the day before the work, and the old joint is cut out to a clean surface. In winter the outer wall tiles are cooler, so the surface there is dried more carefully, and the joint is left to cure before use for as long as the sealant’s manufacturer states.

Why does the joint by the outer wall turn black faster than by an inner wall?

In Jelgava the average outdoor temperature in January is -2.5 °C (LBN 003-19), and in winter the outer wall tiles are cooler than the room air. Moisture condenses on them, and the joint line stays damp longer.

How wide a joint is needed between the bath and the wall?

The width of the joint is set by the gap: the sealant must fill it completely and bond to both the bath edge and the tile. If the gap is wider than the sealant’s manufacturer states, a foam backing cord is put in first, and only then is the silicone laid.

Does the bath panel have to be removed for sealing?

Only if the trap or the overflow has to be checked. The joint between the bath and the wall itself is laid from above, without touching the panel.
