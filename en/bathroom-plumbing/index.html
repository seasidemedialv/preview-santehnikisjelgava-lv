Repairing or renewing a bathroom in Jelgava? The plumber fits everything - from a toilet and basin to a complete shower enclosure or bath.

What bathroom plumbing work in Jelgava includes

  • Toilet fitting: wall-hung toilet or concealed frame (in-wall system), connection to the drain
  • Basin fitting: wall-hung, pedestal or built-in basins
  • Shower enclosure fitting: corner, rectangular and open shower areas
  • Bath fitting and hook-up: acrylic, cast iron or steel baths
  • Mixer tap replacement: kitchen, bath and shower mixer taps
  • Fitting and replacing flat water meters (not the commercial metering unit)
  • Repair of cisterns and flush mechanisms

Bathroom plumbing in Jelgava: a single fitting or the whole room

Fitting bathroom plumbing takes precision - even a small mistake in a connection can cause a leak and damage to the floor or to the ceiling of the flat below.

The plumber fits both single fixtures (toilet swap, basin fitting) and all of the room’s plumbing during a renovation.

A bathroom by zone: toilet, bath, shower corner

By the toilet. A new toilet needs an outlet of at least 100 mm (LBN 221-15). Whether it is a floor-standing toilet or a frame depends on whether the wall is opened: see Toilet fitting. If water runs in the cistern or the base is damp, see Toilet repair; if the cistern is behind a panel, see Concealed cistern toilet repair. For a bidet or a hygiene shower the supply pipes must branch off after the meter: Bidet fitting.

Where the bath is. When a bath is swapped, the distance to the water heater is checked too: for example, Bosch (Tronic 2000T) requires the water heater to be more than 60 cm from the bath, outside protection zones 1 and 2. More on that on the page Bath swap. If a shower will take the bath’s place, you decide whether the floor can be opened and how high the outlet must be raised: Replacing a bath with a shower. The joint between the bath and the wall is renewed by Bath sealing.

In the shower corner. If the house is in the city’s flood-prone area and is connected to the central sewer, the house drain outlet must have a backflow valve (Regulations No. 19-10, point 23); it also protects a cubicle or a drain installed in a basement: Shower enclosure fitting. If the cubicle leaks at the door, the joint or the trap, see Shower enclosure repair; the rail with a rain shower and a thermostat is described in Shower column fitting.

Jelgava consumption norms: 215 l in a house, 150 l in a flat, 90 l without a bath

Annex 1 of Jelgava City binding regulations No. 19-10 sets the water consumption where there is no meter. For a house with central water, sewerage, hot water, baths and showers it is 215 litres a day per resident, in a block of flats 150 l, and in a building without baths and showers 90 l.

In the annex, a building without baths and showers is a separate category. If a bathroom is built from scratch in a house that had none and there is no meter, it is worth finding out how the bill changes: ask SIA Jelgavas ūdens. With a meter the bill follows actual consumption.

In Jelgava Municipality, for example in Ozolnieki, Brankas or Kalnciems, different rules apply: Jelgava Municipality binding regulations No. 2 (2025), and the service provider is SIA Jelgavas novada KU.

Jelgava City binding regulations No. 19-10, Annex 1 and point 23; Jelgava Municipality binding regulations No. 2 (approved 29 January 2025, in force from 9 September 2025). Checked 19 November 2026.

The main streets of Cukurfabrika, Brīvības bulvāris, Lāčplēša iela and Aviācijas iela, took shape in the first period of the Republic of Latvia, and in Aviācijas iela the water supply and sewer were built in 2019. If in a house like this the toilet, the bath (changed for a shower enclosure) and the basin are all swapped at once, the price is worked out as renewing all of the room’s plumbing: from €450 per job, labour price, materials extra.

Questions people ask

What is the starting price for fitting a toilet in Jelgava?

Toilet fitting starts from €50 (unit not included). The price depends on the type of toilet and the condition of the existing pipework.

Do you also fit shower enclosures?

Yes: corner, rectangular and open shower areas. What exactly the work includes, you go through on the call.

Can I fit a mixer tap myself, or should I call a plumber?

Incorrect fitting can lead to a leak and major damage. It is advisable to leave it to a plumber.

How long does bathroom plumbing take to fit?

The plumber tells you the duration on the call, based on the amount of work.

Are the rules for bathroom connections in Jelgava Municipality different from the city?

Yes. In the city, Jelgava City binding regulations No. 19-10 apply, while in the municipality, including Ozolnieki and Kalnciems, Jelgava Municipality binding regulations No. 2 (2025) apply, and the service provider is SIA Jelgavas novada KU. Put specific questions about a connection to your own service provider.

Where should the water heater go so the bath can be swapped later?

Away from the bath. For example, Bosch (Tronic 2000T) requires the water heater to be outside protection zones 1 and 2, more than 60 cm from the bath, in a room above 0 °C and positioned so the anode can be replaced. For other models the requirements are in their instructions.

Why might a basement shower in central Jelgava need a backflow valve?

Regulations No. 19-10 (point 23) require a backflow valve on the house drain outlet if the house is in the city’s flood-prone area and is connected to the central sewer; ask the municipality where this area ends. In the LVĢMC flood risk map, the historic centre is among the areas at risk of spring floods. A basement shower is the lowest point, where water can flow back.
