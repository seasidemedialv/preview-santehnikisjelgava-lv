A burst pipe: first steps at home

If a pipe bursts at home or you see a leak, act without delay

  1. 1

    Close the main water valve

    It is usually by the water meter or in the basement. Turn it clockwise as far as it goes to stop the water flow.

  2. 2

    Mop up the water from the floor

    Use towels, buckets and rags to prevent further damage to floors and walls. If you can, open the windows to ventilate.

  3. 3

    Request an emergency visit

    Fill in the request form with the urgency “ASAP”. Once the form is sent, we contact you within 5 minutes. We also work at night and at weekends.

The left and right bank of the Lielupe, 10 places in the municipality

A plumber in Jelgava works on both banks of the Lielupe. The left bank has the centre with Prospektu gals, Vidus and Vecais gals, as well as the detached-house districts of Līnijas, Miezīte, Romas krogs and Sieramuiža. On the right bank, in Pārlielupe, are the RAF block-of-flats district between Pērnavas iela and Loka maģistrāle, Cukurfabrika, Ozolpils and Langervalde. Pasta sala and Pilssala lie between the Driksa and the Lielupe.

Many detached-house streets have new street networks: in phase V of the project, from 2017 to 2021, 83.49 km of water supply and sewerage networks were built and rebuilt in 100 street sections. The municipality’s 2025 report says that connecting decentralised systems to the new sewer networks continued: 293 contracts were signed with private individuals, 78 of them new connections. The plumbing part is then the yard service pipe and the home’s internal drains; read about it on the page Sewer and water connection for a private house.

Outside the city we work in 10 places in Jelgava Municipality: Ozolnieki, Āne, Tetele, Brankas, Dalbe, Jaunpēternieki, Kalnciems, Mežciems, Kārniņi and Jaunsvirlauka parish. In the municipality, water and sewerage are run by Jelgavas novada KU, and the villages have separate systems: Āne and Tetele share one, Brankas and Jaunpēternieki have one each. All the places are on the page Where we work: Jelgava districts and parishes.

A year in Jelgava: spring floods, 197 heating days and frost

Plumbing in Jelgava changes with the season, because the city is low-lying and crossed by four rivers. The table gives each season’s Jelgava fact, the jobs it usually means and the starting price.

Season What happens in Jelgava Jobs Price
Spring, floods On 15 April 2013 the Lielupe at Jelgava reached 213 cm (flood threshold 260 cm); on 12 March 2026 Bāra ceļš was flooded and closed Drainage and basement pumps, water in the basement visit and look at the problem from €40
Summer, downpours On 30 July 2024, after a downpour, 11 multi-storey residential buildings, yards and basements were under water Clog removal, yard drain line clearing the yard drain line from €50
Autumn, start of heating LBN 003-19: the heating period in Jelgava lasts 197 days, with an average air temperature of 0.7 °C Heating systems, radiator swap radiator swap from €60 each
Winter, frost average temperature of the coldest five days -21.7 °C; clay soil freezes 59 cm deep every 2 years and 119 cm deep every 100 years Frozen pipes, leaks stopping a leak point from €40

Jelgavas ūdens recommends keeping rooms with water supply and sewer pipes at no less than +5 °C in winter and insulating the pipes. For garden houses and summer cottages, the water is drained before frost; how it is done is on the page Winterising the water system of a garden house or summer cottage.

Jelgava's water supply and sewerage in numbers

Elevation
Jelgava lies on a plain only 3.5 m above mean sea level; that is why 32 automatic lifting stations work in the city’s sewerage.
Water supply networks
250,258 m on 1 January 2026, of which 47,501 m are house service pipes, almost a fifth of the network (Jelgavas ūdens).
Water source
groundwater only; since 2014 the city has been supplied by the Tetele well field.
Pressure at the inlet
at least 0.26 MPa, or 2.6 bar, at peak consumption (regulations No. 19-10, point 14).
Leaks on your property
the owner stops them at their own expense (regulations No. 19-10, point 20).

Questions people ask

Do you also work in Jelgava Municipality, not only in the city?

Yes. We serve Jelgava and 10 places in Jelgava Municipality, including Ozolnieki and Kalnciems.

What are the approximate prices of the services?

Finding the fault from €25, plumber’s visit and look at the problem from €40, urgent call-out from €60. On the call you go through the price before the job.

How does the request process work?

Use the form on this page and we contact you within 5 minutes. The time and the job are agreed on the call.

What should I do if a pipe bursts at night or at the weekend?

Close the main water valve straight away. Then fill in the request form with the urgency “ASAP”. We contact you within 5 minutes. We also work at night and at weekends.

Do you offer a free inspection?

Finding the fault (no repair) is from €25. On the call you go through the job and the price before the job.

What materials do you use?

On the call you go through the materials before the job.

In Ozolnieki and Kalnciems, should I contact Jelgavas ūdens about water matters?

No, in the municipality’s villages water supply and sewerage are run by Jelgavas novada KU, while Jelgavas ūdens works in the city of Jelgava. We work on the internal plumbing of a home in both places.

Does the Jelgava municipality co-fund connecting a house to the sewer?

Yes, Jelgava regulations No. 19-2 (point 6) provide co-funding: up to 75 % of the costs for blocks of flats and up to 50 % for single-dwelling houses, if a building contractor builds it. Co-funding is not granted if construction began before the application (point 5.3). The plumbing part is described on the page Sewer and water connection for a private house.
