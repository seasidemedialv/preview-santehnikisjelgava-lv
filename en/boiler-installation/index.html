Where the water heater sits in a Jelgava home

In a flat the water heater is usually in the bathroom or the toilet. The Bosch Tronic 2000T manual requires it to be placed outside bathroom protection zones 1 and 2, that is, more than 60 cm from the bath, and so that the anode can be replaced.

In a house a water heater is often put in a utility room or a basement by the service pipe. Bosch requires a room where the temperature does not fall below 0 °C, while Jelgavas ūdens advises keeping rooms with water pipes at least +5 °C in winter.

If the water heater is already in place but works badly, the job is one of two: water heater descaling (limescale, anode, seal) or water heater repair (heating element, thermostat, safety valve).

Artesian water from the Tetele well field

Jelgava uses only groundwater for drinking water. Since January 2014 the city has been supplied by the Tetele well field: 9 new and 3 rebuilt artesian wells that can deliver 20,736 m³ of water a day, with supply pipelines in 2 lines.

For water heater servicing this is a reference point: the city has one source and the tests published by Jelgavas ūdens. In Ozolnieki and Brankas the water is supplied by Jelgavas novada KU, and the company’s test reports list separate systems, for example for Ozolnieki, Brankas, Āne and Tetele, Kārniņi and Staļģene.

Sources: Jelgavas ūdens description of the water supply system; Jelgavas novada KU water quality reports; checked 19 November 2026.

215 and 150 litres: a billing norm, not a water heater size

Annex 1 of Jelgava City binding regulations No. 19-10 bills water use without a meter at 215 litres per day per resident in a detached house with baths and showers and 150 litres in a block of flats. That is a billing norm for all water, not a rule for choosing a water heater; the size is chosen by how much hot water the family uses.

Water heater connection after the meter: points 15.8 and 15.9

Regulations No. 19-10 forbid making internal water pipe connections before the commercial metering assembly, so a water heater is connected after the water meter; that is point 15.8. Point 15.9 forbids combining a local and a central water supply system: they must be separated by an air gap, and the water heater’s supply pipe must not join them.

On the electrical side, a water heater needs a separate, earthed connection. If the distribution board has none, an electrician builds it.

Questions people ask

How long does water heater installation take in Jelgava?

The duration depends on the connection; the plumber tells you on the call.

What size of electric water heater should I choose?

It depends on the home’s infrastructure and water use. On the call the plumber helps you choose.

Can a new water heater go in the same place as the old one?

Yes, a swap usually takes place in the same spot. A water heater swap including removal of the old one costs from €80 each; disposal of the old unit is agreed on the call. If the new unit differs in size, small pipe adjustments may be needed.

How often should a water heater be serviced in Jelgava?

A preventive service once every 1–2 years is recommended. Jelgavas ūdens does not publish water hardness in its test reports; you can ask the company itself.

What should I do with a water heater in a garden house that is not heated in winter?

For example, Bosch (Tronic 2000T) requires a room where the temperature does not fall below 0 °C. If that cannot be ensured, the water heater is drained before the frost; that is done in the garden house winterising job together with the rest of the water system.

May a water heater be run without a magnesium anode?

No, for example the Bosch (Tronic 2000T) manual forbids it; the anode is checked every year and replaced if necessary. Swapping a water heater element, anode or thermostat costs from €40 per job, labour price, materials extra.
