Tetele wells, the Kalnciems system, the Mežciems borehole: what water is in the water heater

In the city, Jelgavas ūdens has filtered groundwater since 2014 to remove iron and reduces sulphates by nanofiltration: iron is less than 0.1 mg/l, sulphates within 200–250 mg/l.

In the municipality, water is supplied by Jelgavas novada KU, and its test reports list separate systems. In 2016 the Kalnciems system supplied 129 m³ of water a day to about 2,000 consumers, and the water is treated before supply. According to LVĢMC data of 2014, the water of Mežciems (borehole P200395) and Kārniņi (borehole P201023) is supplied without treatment.

JNKU points out that groundwater across the municipality naturally has a raised sulphate content and that heating hard water forms limescale, which can damage plumbing and heating elements. In private houses, local filters to reduce sulphates or hardness can be installed.

Sources: Jelgavas ūdens description of the water supply system; environmental report of the Jelgava Municipality spatial plan; Jelgavas novada KU water quality page. Checked 19 November 2026.

If the water heater is leaking, switch it off at the distribution board and close the cold water valve at the water heater. If the safety valve drips, it is swapped, but if the pressure at the service pipe exceeds the water heater’s working pressure stated in the manual, a pressure reducing valve is fitted before the water heater.

Swapping a water heater element in village water and in the city

The heating element and the thermostat are fixed to a flange, so they are swapped after the water has been drained. If the removed element has a thick layer of limescale, the new one will build up the same in the same water; then it is worth looking at the JNKU test reports for your own system and weighing the filter that the company mentions for private houses.

Bosch requires the Tronic 2000T anode to be checked once a year; in a repair, when the flange is already off, it can be looked at on the same visit. The parts must match the water heater model, so the make and model from the nameplate are useful on the call. Swapping the water heater thermostat is on the same row of the price list, from €40 per job.

In 1984 a 33-flat building was built in Mežciems, and the village water comes from a borehole. Suppose that in one of the flats the water heater is connected to power but the water does not heat, and a look at it reveals a burnt-out heating element under a layer of limescale.

Questions people ask

Where can I find the water tests for Kārniņi or Mežciems?

Jelgavas novada KU publishes drinking water test reports by system; for Jaunsvirlauka Parish they include the Kārniņi and Staļģene water supply system. For the water of the Mežciems borehole, ask the company directly.

May a new water heater be moved closer to the bath?

For example, the Bosch Tronic 2000T must be installed outside bathroom protection zones 1 and 2, more than 60 cm from the bath, in a room that does not fall below 0 °C and where the anode can be replaced. For another maker’s requirements, see its manual.

Does a filter before the water heater protect the element from limescale?

JNKU points out that local filters to reduce sulphates or hardness can be installed in private houses. Whether a filter is needed for your water can be judged from the supplier’s tests and from how much limescale is on the removed element.
