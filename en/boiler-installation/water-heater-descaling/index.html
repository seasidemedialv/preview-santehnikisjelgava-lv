The Tetele well field, nanofiltration and limescale

Jelgavas ūdens uses only groundwater to obtain drinking water. Since January 2014 the city has been supplied by the Tetele well field: 9 new and 3 rebuilt artesian wells that can deliver 20,736 m³ of water a day.

Since May 2014 the water has been filtered to remove iron and sulphates are reduced by nanofiltration; the city is supplied with water with iron below 0.1 mg/l and sulphates within 200–250 mg/l. The company does not state hardness in its monthly reports, and you can ask the company itself for the exact figure.

The Bosch Tronic 2000T manual requires the magnesium anode to be checked every year. On limescale it says that if the cause is raised water hardness, more frequent servicing or water treatment should be considered. How often to clean a water heater can therefore be judged by how much limescale is found at the first cleaning.

Sources: Jelgavas ūdens description of the water supply system and test reports; Bosch Tronic 2000T manual. Checked 19 November 2026.

Bosch: running without a magnesium anode is forbidden

The Bosch Tronic 2000T manual forbids running the water heater without a magnesium anode and requires the anode to be checked every year and replaced if necessary. The anode is reached through the flange, which is why the maker already requires, for installation, a place where the anode can be replaced.

In the price list, swapping an anode is on the row “Water heater element, anode or thermostat swap”, from €40 per job, and the anode itself is a material charged separately. It has to fit this exact model, so the make and model from the water heater’s nameplate are useful on the call.

OpenStreetMap shows 5- and 9-storey blocks of flats on Pērnavas iela. Suppose that in a flat the water heater has not been opened since it was installed, there is a thick layer of limescale at the bottom of the tank, but the anode is still good.

Limescale removal and the anode check come to at least €50. If the anode is worn out, swapping it is a further from €40 per job, at least €90 in total. A new flange seal, if it is replaced, is a material. On the call before the job, the plumber tells you whether the trip is charged separately from the work.

Questions people ask

How hard is the water in Jelgava?

Jelgavas ūdens publishes tests every month covering microbiology, turbidity, electrical conductivity, colour, pH, iron and sulphates, but does not state hardness in them. You can ask the company itself for the exact figure and compare it with the water heater’s manual; more on the page water hardness and your water heater.

Is a conductivity of 742 µS/cm not too high for a water heater?

For example, the Bosch Tronic 2000T permits 130–1,500 µS/cm. The values of 742–783 µS/cm measured in the Jelgava BIOR tests in 2025 and 2026 are within this range; another maker’s limit may differ, so see its manual.

Is limescale also cleaned from the water heater's safety group?

The troubleshooting table of the Bosch Tronic 2000T advises, for limescale in the safety group, removing it and replacing the safety group if necessary. The safety group is on the cold water inlet at the water heater, so it can be looked at on the same visit when the water heater has been drained.
