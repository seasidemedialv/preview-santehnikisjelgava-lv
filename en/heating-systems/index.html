Heating points around the home

Living rooms have radiators under the windows. If a radiator leaks or warms up only partly, that is radiator repair; if it is old or too small for the room, a radiator swap.

In the bathroom a heated towel rail is usually connected to the heating system. There can be water circuits under the floor: new ones are laid in underfloor heating installation, while cold zones and the manifold are fixed in underfloor heating repair.

A house’s utility room has a circulation pump, an expansion vessel and a safety valve. If the radiators stay cold at the bottom throughout the house, the system needs heating system flushing.

City heat on both banks of the Lielupe

More than 600 buildings in Jelgava are connected to the heat supply, including 16,000 households and about 200 businesses (LSM, October 2024). The heat main laid under the Lielupe riverbed in 2012 joined the heating networks on both banks of the river.

The biomass cogeneration plant opened on 11 September 2013 (45 MW of heat, wood chips as fuel) then covered about 85% of the city’s district heating demand. The heat supplier SIA Fortum Jelgava was renamed SIA Gren Jelgava on 5 August 2021. In these buildings the radiator is part of the building’s shared system, not a separate flat’s equipment.

Ozolnieki, Brankas, Āne and Kalnciems: village heating

In Jelgava Municipality, district heating is supplied by SIA Jelgavas novada KU: its price list covers Ozolnieki, Brankas, Āne and Kalnciems, and Brankas also has local heating sites. The 21 December 2020 decision of the regulator (SPRK) approved heat tariffs for Āne and Brankas villages in Cenu parish as well.

Āne has 13 blocks of flats, but private houses dominate, and new ones are still being built in the village. In a house outside the heating network, heating is the owner’s own system with a boiler or heat pump, a circulation pump, an expansion vessel and radiators or underfloor heating; the owner orders that work.

Sources: LSM 23 October 2024; SIA Gren Jelgava (heat main under the Lielupe); Register of Enterprises notice on the name change; Jelgavas novada KU price list and heat energy page; SPRK decision of 21 December 2020; checked 19 November 2026.

Questions people ask

How much does radiator fitting cost in Jelgava?

Radiator fitting starts from €40 (materials not included). On the call you go through the price - it depends on the pipe system and the type of radiator.

Can you install heating in a house in Jelgava?

Yes, in a house the plumber lays the heating pipework and fits the circulation pump and radiators. What exactly the work includes, you go through on the call.

What should I do if a radiator starts to leak?

Close the shut-off valve on that radiator (if there is one) or the main valve of the heating system. Then fill in the form - we contact you within 5 minutes.

How long does installing a complete heating system take?

The plumber gives the duration after a look at the job.

Can a heating system with water be left in an unheated house over winter?

No, if the house is not heated in winter: in Jelgava’s frost a stopped system full of water freezes. It is either drained completely or kept warm; for garden houses that is winterising work.

What should I find out before radiator work in a house with district heating?

When the riser will be shut off and drained. The building manager usually does this, unless the building’s documents say otherwise, so ask them for the time and then fill in the form.
