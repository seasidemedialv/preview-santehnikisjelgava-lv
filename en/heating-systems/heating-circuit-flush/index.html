Houses with their own heat source, in town and in the villages

More than 600 buildings in Jelgava are connected to district heating, including about 16,000 households (LSM, October 2024). The biomass cogeneration plant opened in 2013, whose operator is now SIA Gren Jelgava, then covered about 85% of the city’s district heating demand. In houses not connected to this network, heat comes from their own boiler or heat pump, and the plumber flushes their radiator circuit.

For example, in the detailed plan for 6. līnija 64C, heating on each land plot is to be solved separately, and heat pumps and solar energy solutions are recommended. In the municipality, Jelgavas novada KU supplies district heating in Ozolnieki, Āne and Kalnciems, and in Brankas both district and local heating.

Sources: LSM 23 October 2024 on Jelgava’s heat supply; Fortum 11 September 2013; Register of Enterprises notice on SIA Gren Jelgava; detailed plan for 6. līnija 64C; Jelgavas novada KU price list and heat supply page. Checked 19 November 2026.

The season and the filling water in numbers

Heating period: 197 days
LBN 003-19 (table 7) uses a heating period of 197 days with an average air temperature of 0.7 °C for Jelgava. Planned flushing falls outside it.
Average minimum of the coldest month: -10.3 °C
LBN 003-19 (table 5). If the circuit is flushed in winter, it is refilled and the boiler started in one go, so that nothing freezes in a half-empty circuit.
Iron in the city’s water: less than 0.1 mg/l
That is what is left after the iron removal Jelgavas ūdens has done since May 2014; the filling water brings almost no iron into the circuit.
Sulphates: 200–250 mg/l
The range after nanofiltration (Jelgavas ūdens). In a BIOR sample on 15 September 2026 at Jelgava Pārlielupe Primary School it was 221 mg/l, with an electrical conductivity of 742 µS/cm.
Mežciems and Kārniņi: untreated borehole water
According to the municipality’s environmental report, based on LVĢMC data for 2014, the water in these villages is supplied untreated, while in Kalnciems the water is treated before supply.

The decision that stays with you is the timing: planned work in summer, or work in winter when the circuit is already causing trouble. In the first case the boiler can be left switched off; in the second the work has to be arranged so that the house does not cool down. After flushing, the flow in the circuit changes, so the circuit is usually balanced as well; whether that is part of the job, you go through on the call.

Heating system cleaning: a sample from the bleed valve

The iron in the city’s water is below 0.1 mg/l, so the filling water brings almost no dark particles into the circuit. A sample can be taken into a glass from a radiator bleed valve or the boiler drain tap.

If the water is dark or has sediment from several radiators, that is a reason for heating system cleaning and radiator flushing. If the sample is clear but a radiator warms poorly, the cause may be air or a valve, and then it is best to start with radiator repair.

Tetele wells, a village system or your own borehole: what fills the circuit

Since January 2014 Jelgava has been supplied by the Tetele well field with 9 new and 3 reconstructed artesian wells. In the municipality’s villages Jelgavas novada KU supplies water from separate systems, for example the Ozolnieki, Brankas, or Āne and Tetele systems.

If a house has its own borehole, what filling water the boiler allows is set out in its manual; compare that with an analysis of the borehole water. After filling and the first heat-up, air separates out again, so the radiators are bled once more.

Questions people ask

Can a circuit with aluminium radiators be flushed?

Yes. Any circuit can be flushed with clean water. If a cleaning agent is added to the flush, choose one the radiator manufacturer allows, because aluminium reacts differently from steel.

Is tap water from Kalnciems or Mežciems suitable for filling?

In Kalnciems the water is treated before supply, while in Mežciems and Kārniņi, according to LVĢMC data for 2014, it is supplied untreated. What water the boiler allows is set out in its manual; Jelgavas novada KU publishes water testing reports.

In a house with its own borehole and city water, can the filling pipes be connected?

No. Jelgava City binding regulations No. 19-10 (point 15.9) prohibit combining a local and a centralised water supply system; they must be separated by an air gap. Take the filling line from one system.
