That is where the loop valves, flow meters, mixing unit and pump are, and the fault is often in this assembly. First it is established whether the fault is in one loop or in the whole assembly, and only then is the floor discussed.

When the floor is the only heat: January at -2.5 °C

In table 1 of LBN 003-19 the average January air temperature in Jelgava is -2.5 °C, and February’s is -2.8 °C. In a house where underfloor heating is the only heat source, the manifold cannot be shut off for long in these months. A monthly average is not the lowest value: the average minimum of the coldest month in Jelgava is -10.3 °C (table 5), so work with the floor shut off is planned to be as short as possible.

So before a repair, it is checked whether the manifold has shut-off valves on the flow and the return. If it does, the floor assembly can be isolated while the boiler keeps heating the water heater and the other loops, if there are any. If there are no valves, it is worth fitting them during the first repair, because the next fault will be just as awkward.

Source: Jelgava’s monthly temperatures, building standard LBN 003-19, table 1. Checked 19 November 2026.

A damp floor on the ground: a leak or groundwater

In Jelgava groundwater is high, and in the 2010 floods water began to flow into cellars even of houses in Jelgava’s centre as the groundwater level rose (LV portāls). So a damp patch on a floor on the ground does not always mean a damaged loop.

Before the screed is opened, it is checked whether the system holds pressure. If it does, the damp most likely comes from below, not from the pipe; if it does not, the fault point is located before the floor is opened, and finding the fault is from €25.

Sources: LV portāls on the spring 2010 floods in Jelgava; LLU Landscape Architecture and Art, 2014. Checked 19 November 2026.

Questions people ask

Does damp on the floor by an outer wall always mean a damaged loop?

No. In a floor on the ground the damp can come from below, because groundwater is high on the Jelgava plain. A pressure test helps tell the difference: if the system holds pressure, the pipes are most likely intact.

Can shut-off valves be fitted to the manifold later?

Yes. Then at the next repair only the floor assembly is isolated, and the boiler keeps heating the water heater and the other loops. In a house where the floor is the only heat, shut-off valves shorten the time without heat in January and February.

What should be kept in mind if the manifold is in an unheated room?

Jelgavas ūdens advises keeping rooms with water pipes at least +5 °C in winter and insulating the pipes. A manifold in an unheated extension or garage is the same kind of water point, and on a cold night it can freeze if the floor is not running.
