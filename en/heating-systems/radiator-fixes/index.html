Houses on Gren Jelgava and JNKU heat: repair in the heating season

The city’s heat supplier SIA Fortum Jelgava was renamed SIA Gren Jelgava on 5 August 2021. Its biomass cogeneration plant, opened in 2013, then covered about 85% of the city’s district heating demand.

In the municipality, district heat in Ozolnieki, Brankas, Āne and Kalnciems is supplied by Jelgavas novada KU. In both cases a radiator in a block of flats is part of the building’s system, and if a repair means draining the riser, that is done only with the building manager’s knowledge, while all radiators on that riser go cold for the duration.

Fix it at once or shut it off until spring: temperature and time

The decision is set by the outdoor temperature and by how long the room can stay without a radiator. January averages -2.5 °C, but the average minimum of the coldest month is -10.3 °C, and at a 0.1 probability -15.7 °C (LBN 003-19). If a room by an outer wall can cool below 0 °C on such a night, the water in the radiator and its pipe ends can freeze, and then it is repaired straight away.

If the room stays warm from the rest of the flat, work that needs the riser or circuit drained can be put off until after the heating season: in winter it would mean cold radiators for the neighbours too. After the riser is refilled, air often stays in the radiators, and bleeding air from radiators is from €30 per call-out.

Where it drips and which price line applies: gasket, section, panel

If it drips under the valve stem or at the nut between the valve and the radiator, the fault is usually the gasket, and stopping a drip or leak point is from €40 per job. If water comes between cast iron sections or through the metal of a panel, the radiator is taken apart or swapped; a radiator swap including removal of the old one is from €60 each.

If a radiator warms unevenly even without air, the cause can be sediment, and for that see the page on heating system flushing.

Thermostatic valve: work for after the heating season

Swapping a valve means draining the riser or circuit, so in a block of flats it is better done outside the heating period. LBN 003-19 uses a 197-day period for Jelgava, but when the heating is switched on and off in a particular building is decided by the building manager or the heat supplier.

In a building on city or JNKU heat, first ask the building manager whether the building’s system is designed for thermostatic valves. Fitting a valve is hourly work from €25/h, while the thermostatic head and the valve are materials.

Example: January in Ozolnieki, a dripping pipe connection

In Ozolnieki district heat is supplied by Jelgavas novada KU. Suppose that in January, after a neighbour’s radiator swap, the riser has been refilled, and in a flat in a block the nut between the valve and the radiator starts to drip.

If both radiator valves close, it is isolated from the riser and the joint is resealed, with no need to drain the riser. Stopping the leak from €40 per job and, if another hour of work is needed, hourly work from €25/h: at least €65, materials extra. Before the job, on the call, the plumber says whether the trip is charged separately from the work.

Questions people ask

Who should be told if a radiator leaks in a block on JNKU heat in Ozolnieki?

If a radiator or its valve in a flat leaks, a plumber fixes it; if the riser has to be shut off, the building manager has to know. If there is no heat in the whole building, that is a question for the heat supplier, in Ozolnieki Jelgavas novada KU.

Can a radiator be left shut off when it is -15 °C outside?

For Jelgava, LBN 003-19 puts the average minimum of the coldest month at -15.7 °C at a 0.1 probability. In such weather a room with a shut-off radiator cools by the outer wall, and if it drops below 0 °C, the water in the radiator can freeze. A radiator can be left shut off for a short time if the room stays warm from the rest of the flat.

Can a thermostatic head be fitted in a building on Gren Jelgava heat?

Ask the building manager whether the building’s system is designed for thermostatic valves. If it is, the valve is fitted when the riser can be drained, for example after the heating season, and the head is screwed on afterwards; the valve and the head are materials.
