A floor on the ground 3.5 m above sea level

Jelgava lies on a plain only 3.5 m above mean sea level. An LLU study says that the flat relief and an altitude of 2.0–4.5 m result in high groundwater, and in Ozolnieki the builder of the water management project stated that the groundwater had to be lowered continuously during the works.

In a house with a floor on the ground, this can mean a damp base under the underfloor heating. So before the loops you need to know whether there is a moisture barrier and a sufficient layer of thermal insulation under the floor; without them part of the heat goes into the ground, not the room.

For new houses the city sometimes sets the floor height already in the detailed plan: at 5. līnija 31, where the ground surface is about 3.00 m LAS, the ground floor of homes must be above the flood level with a 10% probability. The layers of the underfloor heating are planned at that height.

Sources: Wikipedia “Jelgava”; LLU Landscape Architecture and Art, 2014, volume 5; Jelgavas novada KU description of EU projects (Ozolnieki); detailed plan for 5. līnija 31, explanatory report 2024. Checked 19 November 2026.

In both cases this is a detached house with its own heat source. The choice is set by what the house already has: if the boiler stays, the floor needs a mixing unit; if the heat source is a heat pump, the manifold is connected to its installer’s diagram.

Laying underfloor heating: pressure test in winter

In Jelgava the average January air temperature is -2.5 °C (LBN 003-19, table 1). In winter the pressure test is done only in a heated room, so that the water in the loops does not freeze.

The loops are filled and kept under pressure while the screed is poured and dries. That way a leak is seen while the pipes are still accessible, and only then is the flow of each loop set at the manifold.

Example: a new detached house in Pūpoliņi, Ozolnieki

The detailed plan “Pūpoliņi” in Ozolnieki, approved on 18 December 2024, provides for detached housing with centralised water and sewage networks. Questions people ask

Is underfloor heating alone enough on the coldest days?

That is judged from the room’s heat loss at the LBN 003-19 average for the coldest five days in Jelgava, -21.7 °C. If the floor does not cover it, a radiator is added to the room, and both are connected to the same heat source.

Is it allowed to heat a floor on the ground in the Lielupe plain?

Yes. Jelgava is about 3.5 m above sea level and groundwater is high, so the most important layers are the moisture barrier and the thermal insulation under the loops. Without them the floor heats the ground.

Can a detailed plan set how high the floor must be?

It can. For example, in the detailed plan for 5. līnija 31, the ground floor of homes must be above the flood level with a 10% probability. Before building a new floor, look at the plan for your plot.
