Mechanical cleaning or chemicals

Unlike the ordinary chemical pipe cleaners sold in shops, professionals use an electric drain snake and a high-pressure water jetting unit. Mechanical cleaning takes the blockage out of the pipe instead of merely thinning it.

Preventive drain maintenance can also be ordered when there is no blockage.

How to recognise a serious blockage

Warning signs: water drains slowly from several places at once, gurgling sounds in the pipes, or a sewer smell in the room. They point to a blocked soil stack, which has to be dealt with without delay so that water does not spill into the flat on the storey below.

When the Jelgavas ūdens office is closed: the boundary and 32 pumping stations

Jelgavas ūdens repairs its networks only for its own customers. The company’s drain-clearing service for times when its office is closed includes an inspection and clarifying the responsibility boundary of the networks: first it establishes whether the blockage is in the company’s network or the owner’s.

In the flat city, the sewer system is kept running by 32 automatic pumping stations. In the sewer network the company has on record (229,685 m, 1 January 2020), house outlets make up 37,860 m, so where the company’s part ends and the owner’s begins for a particular outlet is something Jelgavas ūdens confirms.

If the blockage is in a flat’s drains, in a soil stack or in the house outlet up to the responsibility boundary, it is a plumber’s job. Clearing a blockage in a home’s drains costs from €40 per call-out, clearing a shared soil stack from €80 per call-out, an urgent call-out (emergency) from €60.

In the municipality's villages the boundary is at the first inspection chamber

In villages of the municipality, for example Ozolnieki or Brankas, the sewer is served by Jelgavas novada KU, and Jelgava Municipality binding regulations No. 2 (point 20) put the responsibility boundary at the first inspection chamber of the outlet, counting from the service provider’s network. Where the outlets of several houses are connected in series, the boundary is the first chamber of the first house (point 20.2), and the stretch between neighbouring houses lies beyond it.

In the city, regulations No. 19-10 (point 20) require the owner to maintain the sewer of their property, so a blockage up to the responsibility boundary is the owner’s job. Long and overgrown stretches are cleared by sewer jetting, but if the blockage keeps returning in the same place, consider renewing the drain pipes.

Example: a kitchen outlet in a Lapskalns block of flats

Lapskalns became a district of the city when blocks of flats, detached houses and allotments appeared there. If in a flat in such a building the kitchen sink drains slowly while the bathroom works normally, the blockage is usually in the flat’s own outlet, not in the soil stack. An old, cracked trap is replaced in that case: trap swap.

Clearing a blockage in a flat’s drains costs from €40 per call-out. If the layer of grease in the pipe is thick and is flushed with a high-pressure jet, from €60 per hour (1 h minimum) is added. Questions people ask

Can pipes be cleaned without breaking into walls?

Usually yes: the electric drain snake works through the existing openings, and a wall has to be opened only if the pipe cannot be reached any other way.

What does clog removal cost in Jelgava?

Finding the fault from €25, plumber’s visit and look at the problem from €40. On the call you go through the job and the price with the plumber.

What is the difference between a drain snake and chemical cleaners?

The snake physically breaks up the blockage, while chemicals only dissolve it partly, to varying degrees.

Can the drain clog again the next day?

If the cause is the condition of the pipe, the blockage can return.

May plaster or mortar residue be poured down the drain after renovation?

No. Jelgava City binding regulations No. 19-10 (point 34.2) prohibit discharging lime, sand, plaster, soil, and metal and wood shavings into the sewer. Hardened mortar is not sediment that can be flushed out: usually that section of pipe is replaced.

Does a café in Jelgava need a grease trap?

Yes, if the premises have public catering, food production or another activity whose wastewater contains grease: regulations No. 19-10 (point 24) require it. Confirm with Jelgavas ūdens which trap is needed and where to put it.

May a roof downpipe be connected to the household sewer in Jelgava?

No. Regulations No. 19-10 (point 15.7) prohibit discharging precipitation water into the centralised sewer at will. Point 53, in turn, required users whose rainwater went into the household sewer to rebuild the outlet and build a separate rainwater connection within five years of 2 August 2019. Cleaning does not solve overload in a downpour; ask Jelgavas ūdens about the rebuilding procedure.
