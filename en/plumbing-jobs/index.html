In the city: the Jelgavas ūdens network and 47,501 m of house service pipes

On 1 January 2026 Jelgava’s water supply network was 250,258 m long, of which 47,501 m are house service pipes. What lies on the house side of the responsibility boundary is the home’s own system, and in the city it is set by Jelgava binding regulations No. 19-10: Leak and pipe repair and Clog removal.

Jelgava lies on a plain only 3.5 m above mean sea level, and in the city’s flood-prone area the sewer outlet must have a backflow valve (19-10, point 23): Drainage and basement pumps. For a café or canteen, point 24 requires a grease trap.

In the villages: Jelgavas novada KU and regulations No. 2

In the municipality’s villages, for example Ozolnieki or Kalnciems, Jelgava Municipality binding regulations No. 2 (point 20) put the water supply responsibility boundary of a private house at the property line, and for the sewer at the first inspection chamber on the outlet, counting from the service provider’s network. The yard service pipe, the inlet into the house and the internal plumbing are in the group Plumbing in a private house in Jelgava and around.

Your own septic tank: 3,661 systems in the register

By February 2026 the register of Jelgava’s decentralised sewage systems held 3,661 entries. In 2025 Jelgavas ūdens inspected 534 systems, and 324 of them did not meet the requirements. For such a house the plumbing part is the internal drains up to the outlet and, if there is a network in the street, switching the toilet, shower and basin over to the new outlet.
