Pressure of 2.6 bar at the inlet and a meter 2 m from the wall

Jelgava City regulations No. 19-10 (point 14) set the minimum water pressure at the inlet at ground level at peak consumption: 0.26 MPa, or 2.6 bar. If the shower on an upper floor runs weakly, it is worth comparing the pressure at the inlet with this figure before changing the mixer tap.

A commercial metering unit may be built inside a room if the air temperature is not below +5 °C, the place is easy to reach and lit, and it is at most 2 m from the building’s outer wall (point 13). When pipes are moved near the meter, these requirements still apply.

Connections of the internal water supply before the commercial metering unit are prohibited, and a local and a centralised system must not be joined; they must be separated by an air gap, according to points 15.9 and 15.8.

Jelgava City binding regulations No. 19-10, points 13, 14, 15.9 and 15.8. Checked 19 November 2026.

Connection to the street network: only a licensed company

Regulations No. 19-10 (point 11) say that connecting a water service pipe and a sewer outlet to the centralised networks is done by the service provider or another licensed company under the supervision of the provider’s representative. Before that, the owner requests technical conditions from the service provider (point 5). A handyman plumber’s work is in the house and the yard up to the connection point. In the municipality’s villages, connections and responsibility boundaries are set by Jelgava Municipality binding regulations No. 2, and the service provider is Jelgavas novada KU.

New or rebuilt systems are tested before handover (point 12): a water supply needs a hydraulic test and disinfection certificate, a gravity sewer a pipeline TV inspection report, and a pressure main a hydraulic test certificate. When choosing a plumber for a new build, ask which of these documents applies to your job.

Questions people ask

What is the lowest water pressure Jelgava must provide at a house inlet?

Regulations No. 19-10 (point 14) set 0.26 MPa, or 2.6 bar, at the inlet at ground level at peak consumption.

Can a garden tap be connected before the water meter?

No. Point 15.8 of regulations No. 19-10 prohibits making internal water supply connections before the commercial metering unit.

Which document is needed for a new sewer section before handover?

A gravity sewer needs a pipeline TV inspection report and a pressure main a hydraulic test certificate (point 12 of regulations No. 19-10).
