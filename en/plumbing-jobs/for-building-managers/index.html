Rainwater in the household sewer and smoke testing

Jelgava regulations No. 19-10 (point 53) stated that if rainwater reaches the centralised household sewer, the user had five years from 2 August 2019 to rebuild the outlet and build a new service pipe to the city’s rainwater sewer. That deadline ended on 2 August 2024.

Jelgavas ūdens tests household sewer networks with a smoke generator to find places where rain, soil or drainage water gets into the network, and unauthorised connections. If drainage is connected without construction documents, the company can recover compensation, the amount of which depends on the pipe’s inner diameter.

If a building is in Jelgava’s flood-prone area and is connected to the centralised sewer, a backflow valve must be built into its outlet (point 23). A downpour is a different case: on 30 July 2024 LSM reported that after the downpour of 28 July 11 multi-storey residential buildings in Jelgava were under water, and on Mātera iela pumping went on all day.

Sources: Jelgava City binding regulations No. 19-10, points 23, 24, 39.2, 40.2, 53 and 15.7.; Jelgava Municipality binding regulations No. 2, point 20; Jelgavas ūdens warning about compensation. Checked 19 November 2026.

A plumber for a flat owners' association and its authorised representative

Regulations No. 19-10 (points 39.2 and 40.2) say that in a block of flats the water service contract is concluded by the flat owners, who authorise one person by a decision of the owners, and the management contract or the owners' decision is attached to the contract. The association or its authorised person requests plumbing work in the same way as a building manager, through the form.

Questions people ask

Where does a home's sewer end in Ozolnieki or Kalnciems if several houses' outlets are connected in series?

Jelgava Municipality regulations No. 2 (point 20.2) put the responsibility boundary in this case at the first inspection chamber of the first house, counting from the service provider’s network, unless the building’s documents say otherwise.

Does a building with a café on the ground floor in Jelgava need a grease trap?

Yes, point 24 of regulations No. 19-10 requires grease traps if the premises have public catering or other activity that puts grease into the wastewater.

May a basement drainage pump be connected to the building's household sewer?

Not without approval: point 15.7 of regulations No. 19-10 prohibits discharging groundwater and rainwater into the centralised sewer on one’s own initiative.
