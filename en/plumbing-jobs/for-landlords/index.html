A leak in a rented flat and point 20 of regulations No. 19-10

Jelgava regulations No. 19-10 (point 20) say that the owner or holder of a property is responsible for operating its water supply and sewer systems and stops leaks in them at their own expense. How this is divided between you and the tenant is usually set by the tenancy agreement.

Point 21 requires leaks in your own system to be reported to the water service provider without delay. If you do not live in Jelgava yourself, it is worth writing into the tenancy agreement who does this. Under point 19, the provider is not responsible for water interruptions if the user’s own networks are damaged.

If you do not live in Jelgava yourself, give the tenant a note with what the tenancy agreement says about leaks and reporting. The tenant can also fill in the request form themselves, and once the form is sent, we contact you within 5 minutes.

Jelgava City binding regulations No. 19-10, points 14, 18, 19, 20, 21, 34.2, 34.3 and 38, annex 1. Checked 19 November 2026.

A page for the tenant: what must not go down the drain in Jelgava

Regulations No. 19-10 (points 34.2 and 34.3) prohibit discharging into the sewer solid objects, textiles, lime, sand, plaster, soil, oils, grease, metal and wood shavings, and unground food waste. This list can be attached to the tenancy agreement or put up in the bathroom. The tenant can wipe grease and oil from a pan with paper and put it in the rubbish.

Questions people ask

How much water is charged in Jelgava if a rented flat has no meter?

Annex 1 of regulations No. 19-10 sets 150 l a day per resident for a block of flats, and 90 l for a building without baths and showers.

Must the new tenant conclude a contract with Jelgavas ūdens themselves?

Point 38 applies to whoever becomes the owner or holder of the property: within 5 working days they must submit a certificate with the meter reading or conclude a contract. Whether the tenant is a holder in your case, ask Jelgavas ūdens.

Why must wet wipes not be flushed down the toilet?

Wet wipes are fabric that does not break down; point 34.2 of regulations No. 19-10 prohibits textiles in the sewer. At treatment plants screens with 3 mm gaps remove rags, but before that they can already block the house pipe.
