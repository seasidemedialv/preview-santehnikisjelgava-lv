Everything for the basement is gathered here: the pump pit, drainage pump repair, a backflow valve in the outlet and a sewage pump for a basement toilet.

The basement by place: floor pit, outlet, toilet, discharge pipe

Floor pit. A drainage pump stands in a pit at the lowest point of the basement and is switched on by a float switch. For its Drain TMW 32 model, the manufacturer Wilo states that it pumps rainwater and drainage water with a small amount of sand, and that pumping sewage and oils is prohibited. When the pump no longer switches on or off: drainage pump fixes.

Water on the floor. If water appears in a basement after spring floods or a downpour, first find out whether it comes from the ground or from the sewer. The solution depends on it: a pit with a pump, or a backflow valve in the outlet. Both routes are described on the page about water in the basement.

Toilet, shower or washing machine in the basement. If the appliance sits lower than the street sewer, the wastewater is lifted with a pump. When such a pump stops, hums or smells: sewage pump fixes.

Discharge pipe from the pump. It carries water from the pit to a discharge point outside the household sewer. If it runs through an unheated basement, an outer wall or a yard, in Jelgava it has to be protected from frost, and it is checked before winter.

The flood plain and point 23 of regulations No. 19-10

Jelgava City binding regulations No. 19-10 (point 23) state that if a property lies in the city’s flood plain and is connected to the centralised sewer, a backflow valve must be built into its sewer outlet. In the LVĢMC flood risk plan, flooding of the Lielupe flood plain in Jelgava begins at 1.55 m LAS, and flooding of the city’s lowest areas at 2.65 m LAS. In spring 2010, because of an ice jam, the level reached 3.47 m LAS, and at such a level about a quarter of Jelgava’s territory is flooded.

The other limit runs in the opposite direction. Regulations No. 19-10 (point 15.7) prohibit discharging groundwater and precipitation water into the centralised sewer at will. Jelgavas ūdens states that for drainage connected to a sewer outlet without documentation, it may calculate compensation depending on the inner diameter of the pipe. Water from a drainage pump needs its own route.

In Jelgava Municipality, the head of the municipal council named the most vulnerable places in March 2026: the Lielupe on the Kalnciems and Staļģene side, the Jelgava side where allotments have been converted into residential zones, and Ūziņi by the Svēte.

Sources: Jelgava City binding regulations No. 19-10, LVĢMC flood risk management plan for the Lielupe basin, Jelgavas ūdens, LSM 5 March 2026; checked on 19 November 2026.

Building a pump pit in a basement includes a pit in the floor, a pump with a float switch, and a discharge pipe with a gate valve and a backflow valve up to an approved discharge point. The price of fitting a basement or drainage pump depends on the amount of work, access and materials.

A basement toilet: pump in a pit or a compact unit behind the toilet

If a basement toilet or shower is lower than the lid of the inspection chamber, the wastewater is lifted by a sewage pump in a pit or by a compact unit right behind the toilet, which on the market is often called Sololift after the Grundfos brand. In the city’s flood plain the outlet also needs a backflow valve (point 23). The price of fitting a sewage pump depends on the amount of work, access and materials.

Example: a basement in Ģintermuiža by the Svēte

On the LVĢMC maps, the Ģintermuiža (later hospital) development is among the sites at risk of flooding. In the 2010 spring floods the Svēte was one of the first rivers to burst its banks. A private house owner in Ģintermuiža wants to know first, after the floods, where the basement water comes from: through the floor or along the outlet.

Questions people ask

How deep must the discharge pipe of a drainage pump lie in a Jelgava yard?

In the Jelgava row of the building climatology standard LBN 003-19, frost reaches 59 cm in clayey soil with a return period of once in 2 years and 119 cm once in 100 years; deeper in sandy soil. Wilo requires the discharge pipe to be frost-protected, so its depth is chosen by the ground in the particular yard.

Is a backflow valve mandatory in Ozolnieki and Kalnciems too?

Regulations No. 19-10 apply to the city of Jelgava. In the municipality, Jelgava Municipality binding regulations No. 2 are in force, and the service provider is Jelgavas novada KU: ask it about a backflow valve at a particular site. LBN 221-15 (point 259) requires appliances below the lid of the nearest inspection chamber to have a separate outlet and a device against backflow wherever the house is.

What does it mean for Ūziņi by the Svēte that the river can burst its banks within three hours?

That the pump has to be ready before the floods. If a pump is not used for a long time, Wilo recommends for its Drain TMW 32 running one pumping cycle every 2 months to make sure it switches on.
