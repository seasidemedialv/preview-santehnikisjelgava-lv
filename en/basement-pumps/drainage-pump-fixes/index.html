Where the pump's water goes: regulations No. 19-10 and the smoke test

A repair also looks at the other end of the hose. Regulations No. 19-10 (point 15.7) prohibit discharging groundwater and precipitation water into the centralised sewer at will, so a drainage pump may not be connected to the household sewer without approval.

Jelgavas ūdens tests household sewer networks with a smoke generator, looking for places where drainage water enters them, and for unauthorised connections. For drainage connected to an outlet without documentation, the company may calculate compensation, the amount of which depends on the inner diameter of the pipe.

If the hose of an old pump has for years been led into a basement floor drain, a repair is the moment to change that connection. For rainwater, the regulations name the city’s drainage and storm sewer system as the destination; where exactly your discharge pipe may be connected, ask Jelgavas ūdens.

Sources: Jelgava City binding regulations No. 19-10 (points 15.7., 53), Jelgavas ūdens (smoke tests, warning about compensation); checked on 19 November 2026.

After spring floods the pit is cleaned first and only then is the pump itself assessed: Wilo allows its Drain TMW 32 to handle drainage water only with a small amount of sand. Questions people ask

Can a drainage pump freeze in an unheated Jelgava basement in winter?

It can. Wilo requires its Drain TMW 32 to be fully submerged once installed, with the liquid and ambient temperature at least +3 °C. LBN 003-19 gives Jelgava an average temperature of -21.7 °C for the coldest five days, so a basement without heating is checked before winter.

May a drainage pump be used for shower or washing machine water?

Wilo allows it for the Drain TMW 32: it pumps wastewater without faeces from sinks, showers, baths and washing machines, at a liquid temperature of 3–40 °C. This water is household wastewater, so it goes into the household sewer, unlike drainage water.

How often does a drainage pump need servicing?

For its Drain TMW 32, Wilo recommends a service check after 1,500 operating hours. If a pump stands idle for a long time, one cycle is run every 2 months to check that it still switches on.
