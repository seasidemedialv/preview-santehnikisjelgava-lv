A repair checks both the pump itself and the backflow valve in the outlet and, in an unheated sauna, whether the water in the tank has frozen. The plumber’s visit and look at the problem costs from €40.

A city that pumps for itself: 32 stations and 17,877 m of pressure mains

In Jelgava, wastewater cannot reach the treatment plant by fall alone. Because the terrain is flat, the city has 32 automatic pumping stations, and at the start of 2020 the network had 17,877 m of pressure mains (Jelgavas ūdens).

A house pump is the same system on a smaller scale. If a basement toilet is lower than the street network, the wastewater has to be lifted, and in Jelgava’s flood-prone area the outlet must also have a backflow valve (regulations No. 19-10, point 23). So a repair checks both: the pump and the valve.

Sources: Jelgavas ūdens (Sewer system), Jelgava City binding regulations No. 19-10; checked on 19 November 2026.

Diameters, screens and frost limits

Toilet outlet
at least 100 mm (LBN 221-15, annex 3). A pump unit replaces the fall of a gravity drain with a pressure pipe, so its discharge is narrower than the toilet outlet.
Screens at the city treatment plant
3 mm gaps remove rags, sand and grease (Jelgavas ūdens). A house pump unit has no such screens.
A room with pipes in winter
at least +5 °C, recommended by Jelgavas ūdens; this applies also to the room where the pump tank stands.
Average of the coldest five days
-21.7 °C (LBN 003-19, table 6). In an unheated sauna on such days, water left in the discharge pipe can freeze too.

Repair or replace: the decision comes down to the motor

After the impeller is cleaned and the check valve inspected, one thing decides: whether the motor turns. If it does, the unit is repaired and put back, hourly work from €25/h. If the winding is damaged or the casing cracked, the unit is replaced, and the new one is chosen by how many appliances are connected to it: only the toilet, or also a shower and a washing machine. The price of fitting a sewage (lifting) pump depends on the amount of work, access and materials.

Sewage pump repair in a basement pit

In a sewage pump in a basement pit, the impeller can jam between the vanes and the casing, and then the pump stops with a hum. Before a repair, the backflow valve on the discharge pipe is checked too, because if it does not close, water runs back from the pipe into the pit.

Before the pump is lifted out, the pit is pumped empty and the gate valve on the discharge pipe is closed, so that water does not run back from the pipe. If the impeller is intact, the pump keeps working after cleaning; if the vanes are broken, they are replaced or the whole pump is replaced.

A sauna or summer house in winter: a frozen tank

Jelgavas ūdens recommends keeping rooms with water supply and sewer pipes at least +5 °C in winter and insulating the pipes. In a sauna or summer cottage left unheated over the winter, water standing in the pump tank and discharge pipe can freeze and crack the casing.

If a unit is replaced after frost, the new one is chosen by the connected appliances, and a check is made that no water is left in the discharge pipe. The price of fitting a sewage (lifting) pump depends on the amount of work, access and materials.

Example: a unit behind a basement toilet in Pogu lauki

In Pogu lauki, Phase V, section 29 provided for a gravity sewer with a sewage pumping station and a pressure main, and the networks were put into service in 2019, so the street network already has a pumping station. If in the basement of such a house the unit behind the toilet hums but does not pump, the tank is opened and the impeller cleaned.

The plumber’s visit and look at the problem costs from €40, and cleaning the tank is hourly work from €25/h. With two hours of work, at least €90 in total, labour price, materials extra.

Questions people ask

May wet wipes be flushed down a toilet with a pump unit behind it?

No. Regulations No. 19-10 prohibit textiles in the sewer, and at the city treatment plant rags are caught by screens with 3 mm gaps. A house unit has no such screens, and the wipes wind around the impeller.

In Jelgava's flood-prone area, does a basement pump unit also need a backflow valve in the outlet?

Regulations No. 19-10 (point 23) require a backflow valve in the sewer outlet if the house lies in the city’s flood-prone area and is connected to the centralised sewer. The requirement applies to the whole outlet, not only to the pump, so a repair checks both.

Why does a pump unit switch on by itself when nobody is using anything?

Usually water slowly runs into the tank from a toilet cistern that does not seal, or back along the discharge pipe if the backflow valve does not close. Both are checked before the pump is repaired.
