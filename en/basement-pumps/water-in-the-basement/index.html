3.5 m above sea level: spring floods, downpours and full networks

Jelgava lies on a plain only 3.5 m above mean sea level. On 15 April 2013 the water level of the Lielupe at Jelgava reached 213 cm against a flood limit of 260 cm, and several streets were flooded, mainly because of high water in the Lielupe’s tributaries.

A downpour does the same in a shorter time. On 30 July 2024, LSM reported that in Jelgava 11 multi-storey residential buildings stood in water, yards, basements and foundations were still flooded, and in private houses pumping in many places did not help because there was nowhere to pump to.

That is why for a basement pump the place to discharge the water is chosen first, and only then the pump itself. If the whole area is under water, a pump without a discharge point only moves the water around the yard.

A backflow valve in the flood plain: point 23

If water comes into a basement from a floor drain or toilet rather than through the floor, it is backflow from the street network. Point 23 of regulations No. 19-10 requires a backflow valve in the sewer outlet in Jelgava’s flood plain for properties connected to the centralised sewer.

For basement appliances whose level is below the lid of the nearest inspection chamber, LBN 221-15 (point 259) requires their own outlet and a device against backflow outside the flood plain too. Appliances above the chamber lid are not covered by this requirement.

A sewer backflow valve is placed where it can be opened and cleaned: in the basement outlet before the wall, or in the inspection chamber. While the street network is full and the valve is closed, the basement appliances are not used, because their water has nowhere to go.

In the municipality: a meter well in high groundwater (point 21.2)

Jelgava Municipality binding regulations No. 2 (point 21.2) allow a meter assembly to be built outside the ownership boundary at an agreed place if a raised groundwater level at the boundary could damage the meter or make it hard to read and replace.

If the inlet well with the meter floods every spring, ask Jelgavas novada KU about moving the assembly; the plumber then carries out the rebuilding of the service pipe.

Example: a basement in Kalnciems after a downpour

In the July 2024 downpour, allotments and properties flooded in almost all of Kalnciems Parish, basements above all. If in such a house the basement water came from the floor drain, the job is a backflow valve in the outlet; if through the floor, a pit with a pump.

Questions people ask

Why did pumping not help in many Jelgava private houses in the 2024 downpour?

On 30 July 2024, LSM wrote that in private houses pumping in many places did not help because there was nowhere to pump to, and that the rainwater pumping stations could not discharge the large volume of water. That is why the discharge point for a pump’s pipe is chosen before the pump, not after it.

Is a backflow valve needed if the house is not in the flood plain?

Point 23 of regulations No. 19-10 applies to the city’s flood plain. But for basement appliances whose level is below the lid of the nearest inspection chamber, the building standard LBN 221-15 (point 259) requires a device against backflow wherever the house is.

How powerful a pump is needed if water comes into the basement quickly?

The pump’s capacity has to be greater than the inflow. For example, Wilo states up to 10 m³/h for the Drain TMW 32/8 and up to 14.5 m³/h for the TMW 32/11. The inflow is assessed on a visit, by how quickly the pit fills.
