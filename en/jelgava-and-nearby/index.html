Below they are split by Lielupe bank and by parish; each place has its own page. In the official 2026 division the city has 17 districts with different borders and names, but this page uses the 21 familiar names.

Lielupe left bank: 15 districts

The left bank has the old town with Prospektu gals, Vidus and Vecais gals, as well as detached-house and allotment districts from Līnijas to Sieramuiža. Sieramuiža includes 31 streets, more than any other district.

The left bank also has Lapskalns, where the city’s wastewater treatment plant is at Lapskalna iela 22, and Jaunais gals, Ģintermuiža, Miezīte, Romas krogs, Lediņi, Dienvidu gals and Viskaļi.

Beyond the Svēte river are Būriņu dārzi and Pogu lauki; railways separate Pogu lauki from the rest of the city on two sides. In the official 2026 division both belong to the district of Ģintermuiža-Būriņi.

Pārlielupe and the islands: 6 districts

The right bank has Rafs, Cukurfabrika, Ozolpils, Langervalde and Cepļi. Rafs is a block-of-flats district named after the RAF factory; the Riga bus factory at Aviācijas iela 18 operated from 1975 to 1998. Ozolpils is a district of the city, not a parish of the municipality. In Cepļi, in section 28 of phase V, the water supply and sewer were commissioned at the end of 2018.

Between the Driksa and the Lielupe is the district of Salas with Pasta sala and Pilssala; it elects no representative to the residents' council.

Jelgava Municipality: 4 parishes, 10 places

Cenu parish has Āne, Tetele, Brankas, Dalbe and Jaunpēternieki; the parish has no administration of its own, and its service centres are in Brankas and Āne. Ozolnieki parish has only one populated place, Ozolnieki, and the parish covers 8.0 km².

Kalnciems is the centre of Kalnciems parish, and from 1991 to 2010 it was a town. Jaunsvirlauka parish, with its centre in Staļģene, has Mežciems, Kārniņi and Jaunsvirlauka village.

Water from a borehole in Mežciems and Kārniņi

In the municipality’s villages, water and sewerage are run by Jelgavas novada KU. The water source for Mežciems and Kārniņi is a borehole, and according to LVĢMC’s 2014 data the water was supplied there untreated. Kalnciems water is treated before supply. The environmental report of the municipality’s territory plan lists a central water supply in Jaunsvirlauka village but no centralised sewerage.
