Riser renewal in a flat in Jelgava - sections by storey

Renewing a riser in a flat in Jelgava is a rebuild of the building’s system, and two requirements of the city regulations affect it before the work starts: no branch may remain in the basement at the service pipe before the meter, and rebuilt systems have tests before being put into operation.

In the basement at the service pipe: points 15.8 and 12

The regulations forbid making internal water pipe connections before the commercial metering assembly. In a block of flats that is usually at the building’s service pipe in the basement, so when the lower part of a riser is re-laid, the new section starts after this assembly, and an old branch to a basement tap before it is not restored. The rule is in regulations No. 19-10, point 15.8.

Point 12 of the regulations requires tests for new or rebuilt systems before they are put into operation; for a water pipe these are a hydraulic test and a disinfection report. Whether that applies to the riser work in your building, ask the building manager or Jelgavas ūdens before the work, not after it.

If the building is being renovated, riser work can be included in the project; about that, see the page What changes in a flat’s plumbing when a building is renovated.

Source: text of Jelgava regulations No. 19-10 on likumi.lv (points 12, 14 and 15.8), viewed 19 November 2026.

The flat's water pipes and drains behind the new section

Once the riser has been renewed on its storey, pipe renewal in the flat starts at its branch and the flat’s meters. A bathroom with a basin, bath, toilet and washing machine has six connections to the water pipes (cold and hot for the basin and the bath, one each for the toilet and the machine) and four to the drains.

One water or drain connection point costs from €35, and renewing a drain branch in a flat from €30 per outlet. Six water connections are at least €210, four drain points at least €120, labour price. If the flat’s pipes are renewed on the same visit, the riser is shut off once, not twice.

Questions people ask

Whom do I ask about risers in a building managed by Jelgavas nekustamā īpašuma pārvalde?

In 2010 LV portāls wrote that Jelgavas nekustamā īpašuma pārvalde is the largest building manager in Jelgava and at the time looked after 435 residential buildings with about 14,500 flats. For shutting off a riser and the decision procedure, ask your own building’s manager directly.

Who signs the water service contract in a block of flats?

Regulations No. 19-10 (points 39.2 and 40.2) provide that the contract is concluded by the flat owners, authorising one person by a decision of the owners' community, with the management contract or the community’s decision attached. For building-wide work, such as renewing a whole riser, ask the building manager or the authorised person.

Do 464 series buildings on Meiju ceļš have a basement?

Yes. In the Ministry of Economics study, the 464 series buildings studied have five storeys above ground and a basement storey. In such a building the water service pipe and the riser shut-offs are usually in the basement, so access there is needed on the day of the work too.
