Frozen pipes in Jelgava - service pipe, meter chamber, outdoor tap

Frozen pipes in Jelgava are usually where a room or the ground cools below zero: an unheated basement, garage, veranda, meter chamber or a service pipe by the outer wall; Jelgavas ūdens advises keeping at least +5 °C in such rooms.

+5 °C in rooms with pipes: Jelgavas ūdens advice and point 13

Jelgavas ūdens advises keeping rooms that carry water supply and sewerage pipes at least +5 °C in winter and insulating the pipes. For the meter assembly this is a regulatory requirement: Jelgava regulations No. 19-10 (point 13) allow it indoors only where the air is no colder than +5 °C, the spot is accessible and no further than 2 m from the outer wall.

In a house, the places that most often fall below this limit are a basement with ventilation hatches, a garage, an added-on veranda, a sauna and a garden house. The pipe freezes first there, not in the living rooms.

The stretch where the service pipe comes out of the ground and passes through the foundations is the coldest spot in the whole system. If the meter assembly is 2 m from the outer wall, this stretch is short and can be insulated along its whole length.

Sources: Jelgavas ūdens winter advice; Jelgava City binding regulations No. 19-10, point 13. Checked 19 November 2026.

Service pipe frozen: on the street side or beyond the boundary

If water is missing in the whole house but the neighbours have it, the ice is in your service pipe. If water is missing for the whole street, the street network is frozen or damaged, and it is reported to the water supplier: Jelgavas ūdens in the city, Jelgavas novada KU in the municipality.

Jelgava Municipality regulations No. 2 (point 20) put the water supply responsibility boundary for a private house at the property boundary or the connection point, and for a block of flats at the outer wall. Ice beyond that boundary is thawed by a plumber; before it, that is the company’s network.

Municipal regulations, sub-point 21.2: a chamber behind the fence and a burst from frost

In the municipality, the meter assembly may be built outside the ownership boundary at an agreed location if high groundwater on the boundary could damage the meter (regulations No. 2, sub-point 21.2). In Ozolnieki, the builders of the village networks had to keep lowering the groundwater continuously. In such a chamber, ice can be in the meter or in the pipe from the chamber to the house.

The chamber is insulated under the lid for winter, and the lid is kept closed. If the underground water pipe between the chamber and the house has frozen, it is thawed through the pipe from the house side.

Ice expands and can split a pipe, a joint or the meter body in the chamber. Before the frozen section is heated, close the service valve: once the ice melts, water comes through the crack at full mains pressure, in the city at least 2.6 bar at the service pipe (regulations No. 19-10, point 14).

If frost has damaged the water meter, report it to the supplier: Jelgavas ūdens in the city, JNKU in the municipality, which also seals meters. If a pipe has burst from frost, that is a pipe section repair from €50, with an urgent call-out from €60.

Outdoor tap installation and the garden watering meter

The price of an outdoor tap through the wall depends on the amount of work, access and materials.

Jelgavas ūdens installs an additional meter for its subscribers for water that does not go into the sewer, for example for watering the garden, and only the company’s authorised staff seal it. When an outdoor tap is fitted on such a line, the meter stays sealed by the company, and it too has to be protected from frost for winter. For garden houses and summer cottages: winterising the water system.

Example: a frozen meter chamber in Ozolnieki

Suppose that in Ozolnieki a family house has its meter chamber behind the fence, and after several frosty nights the house has no water, although the neighbours do. The ice is in the chamber and in the pipe to the house, not in the street network.

An urgent call-out is from €60; the thawing price depends on the amount of work, access and materials. If a joint in the chamber has split, a pipe section repair from €50 is added, at least €110 in total plus the thawing. Labour price, materials extra.

Questions people ask

Whom do I report to if the water pipe is frozen on the street side in Ozolnieki?

The street network in Ozolnieki is Jelgavas novada KU’s responsibility, and for a private house the responsibility boundary is the property boundary or the connection point (municipal regulations No. 2, point 20). If it is frozen before that, report it to the company; beyond it and beyond the meter, a plumber thaws the service pipe.

Is +2 °C enough in the meter room in winter?

No. Jelgava regulations No. 19-10 (point 13) allow the meter assembly indoors only where the air is no colder than +5 °C, and Jelgavas ūdens advises the same minimum for all rooms with pipes.

May I remove the garden watering meter for winter myself?

The additional meter for water that does not go into the sewer is sealed only by Jelgavas ūdens authorised staff. Do not touch the seal: the water is drained from the line with the valve and the drain tap, and for removing the meter ask the company.
