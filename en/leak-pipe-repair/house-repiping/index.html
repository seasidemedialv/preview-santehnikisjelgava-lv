The service pipe in numbers: 2 years, 2 m and the frost line

Technical specifications for the connection
valid for 2 years (regulations No. 19-10, points 5 and 8).
Meter assembly indoors
no further than 2 m from the outer wall, in a room no colder than +5 °C (point 13).
Frost depth in clay soil
from 59 cm in a winter that recurs every 2 years to 119 cm in a winter that recurs every 100 years; more in sandy soil (LBN 003-19, table 15).
Pressure at the service pipe
at least 0.26 MPa, or 2.6 bar (point 14).
Testing before operation
for a new or rebuilt water pipe, a report of hydraulic testing and disinfection (point 12).

A service pipe on your own land: the simplified procedure and who connects to the network

If a branch of the street network has already been built up to your boundary, Jelgavas ūdens construction specialists can draw up a service pipe layout plan under Cabinet of Ministers regulations No. 253, and the service pipe on your own land can then be built under a simplified procedure. You need an application and copies of the land register certificate and the boundary plan.

The connection to the central network itself is made by the service provider or a licensed company under its representative’s supervision (regulations No. 19-10, point 11), the company’s representative accepts the connection, and a service contract has to be concluded afterwards. The plumber’s job is the pipe in the yard up to the house and everything behind the meter assembly.

Replacing old metal pipes in Ozolnieki and elsewhere in the municipality

Jelgavas novada KU points out that groundwater across Jelgava Municipality naturally has a raised sulphate content, which can promote corrosion of metal water pipes, including a house’s internal ones, and advises paying attention to the condition of the pipes. This applies to the Ozolnieki, Āne and Tetele, Brankas and other village systems.

In the city, Jelgavas ūdens has reduced sulphates by nanofiltration since 2014 and supplies water with sulphates in the range of 200–250 mg/l. Old steel pipes, both in the city and in the municipality, are usually swapped for plastic or multilayer pipes, which do not rust.

Renewing the water pipes in a house: a wooden house on Vecpilsētas iela or a post-war family house

Around Vecpilsētas iela, part of the wooden buildings of the 18th and 19th centuries has survived. In such a house the pipes run through wooden walls and floors, and the fixings go into load-bearing parts, not into the cladding.

In Pārlielupe, dense plots of private houses were laid out in the 1950s and 1960s along Rīgas iela, Garozas iela, Kalnciema iela and Brīvības iela, and a private-house area of 27 ha in Langervalde. On Kalnciema ceļš the Phase V water main and sewer were put into operation at the end of 2020, so the pipes in a house may have been laid at a different time from the service pipe.

If an old well is left in the yard, its pipes must be separated from the city water main by an air gap (regulations No. 19-10, point 15.9).

Example: Apšu iela in Cepļi after the new main

In 2018 a water main was built on Apšu iela from No. 16 to Birzes iela and from No. 27 to No. 35. Suppose that on this stretch a house needs 12 m of service pipe from the boundary to the outer wall and the water pipes re-laid to the kitchen sink, bath, basin, toilet, washing machine and water heater: 10 connection points in all, because the sinks, the bath and the water heater have both cold and hot water.

12 m, priced from €10/m, gives at least €120, and 10 points, priced from €35 each, give at least €350, so at least €470 in total. Labour price, materials extra; whether digging the trench is part of the job, you go through on the call.

Questions people ask

My street was in Phase V: where do I start with connecting?

With a request for technical specifications to the Technical Department of Jelgavas ūdens: you need an application, the land register certificate and a copy of the land boundary plan. The technical specifications issued are valid for 2 years (regulations No. 19-10, point 8).

May I keep the old well alongside the city water main?

Yes, but not connected. Regulations No. 19-10 (point 15.9) forbid combining a local and a central water supply system, and they must be separated by an air gap. The well can be used, for example, for watering, through separate pipes.

Why does Jelgavas novada KU advise paying attention to metal pipes?

The company points out that groundwater in the municipality naturally has a raised sulphate content, which can promote corrosion of metal pipes in a house as well. In private houses, local filters to reduce sulphates or hardness can also be installed.
