Visit from €40, inspection from €25, emergency from €60

The plumber’s visit and look at the problem is from €40 per visit. If you only need to find out what is faulty, without a repair, finding the fault is from €25 per inspection. An urgent call-out in an emergency is from €60.

Whether the trip is charged separately from the work, the plumber tells you on the call before the job starts. The prices in the table are the same in Jelgava and in the municipality’s villages.

A plumber by the hour, and small jobs without a row of their own

Hourly work from €25/h is for small jobs that have no row in the table, for example tightening a joint. If the job does have a row, for example a mixer tap swap from €30 each, you decide on the call before the job which calculation to use. More about small jobs on the page Handyman plumber.

A connection with municipal co-funding: the order before the estimate

Jelgava regulations No. 19-2 (point 6) provide that the municipality co-funds connecting homes to the sewer: up to 75 % of the costs for blocks of flats and up to 50 % for single-dwelling houses, if a building contractor builds it. If the owner builds the connection on their own land, the support is calculated for each metre built, up to the maximum amount set in the regulations.

  1. Application before the work Co-funding is not granted if the connection is already built or construction began before the application (point 5.3). So do not start digging until the application has been submitted.
  2. Estimate against the market average The commission may reject an application if the construction costs exceed the market-research average by more than 30 % (point 13.2). It is worth comparing the estimate with the market average before applying.
  3. Work inside the house Once the outlet to the house is ready, the fixtures are switched over to the new outlet. This part is in our table: a single water or drain connection point from €35 per outlet.

An example in Cepļi, where in section 28 of phase V the water supply and sewer were commissioned at the end of 2018. A detached house moves from its own system to the street network, and inside the house the toilet, shower and kitchen sink are switched over to the new outlet: 3 × €35, at least €105 in total. Digging and the service pipe to the street are not in this sum.

Jelgava City binding regulations No. 19-2 (version from 4 April 2024), points 6 and 13.2, and point 5.3. Checked 19 November 2026.

Questions people ask

What share of the sewer connection costs in Jelgava can the municipality co-fund?

Regulations No. 19-2 provide up to 75 % for blocks of flats and up to 50 % for single-dwelling houses, if a building contractor builds it. If construction began before the application, co-funding is not granted.

What does the 30 % limit in the estimate mean for connection co-funding?

Point 13.2 of regulations No. 19-2 lets the commission reject an application if the construction costs exceed the market-research average by more than 30 %. It is worth comparing the estimate before applying, not after.

Are the table prices different in Ozolnieki or Kalnciems?

No, the job rows are the same. Whether the trip is charged separately from the work, the plumber tells you on the call before the job.
