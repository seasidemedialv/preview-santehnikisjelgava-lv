If the water for a house in Dalbe comes from its own well or borehole, the main plumbing decision is how deep to lay the pipe to the house and where it comes above ground.

Dalbe is a small village of Cenu parish on the bank of the river Misa, about 14 km from the centre of Jelgava (our reading of the map), with a fieldstone church of 1869 between the road V1066 and the Misa, and the “Dalbe” railway halt.

Frost depth: the LBN 003-19 rows for Jelgava

The Latvian building standard LBN 003-19 (table 15) gives, for Jelgava, the standard frost depth of clayey soil by how often such a depth recurs. This row serves as a guide for Dalbe too:

Recurs Clayey soil What it means for a pipe
once in 2 years 59 cm a pipe at a depth of 60 cm can feel frost in roughly every second winter
once in 5 years 79 cm by the standard, frost reaches this depth once in five years
once in 10 years 91 cm by the standard, frost reaches this depth once in ten years
once in 20 years 100 cm about a metre below the surface
once in 50 years 111 cm by the standard, frost reaches this depth once in fifty years
once in 100 years 119 cm the greatest value in the table

For sandy soil the LBN 003-19 values are greater than for clay. So before digging the route, find out what the soil in your own yard is, and choose the row by how much risk you are ready to take. The final depth is also limited by where the service pipe enters the house and by the height of the source or connection.

The Latvian building standard LBN 003-19 “Building climatology”, tables 1, 3, 5, 6 and 15, likumi.lv, checked on 19 November 2026.

Where the water pipe comes above ground

A deep route does not help if the pipe freezes where it rises: at the foundation, in an unheated porch or at an outdoor tap. For Jelgava, LBN 003-19 gives an average temperature of the coldest five days of -21.7 °C (table 6), an absolute minimum of -34.9 °C in February (table 3) and an average minimum air temperature of the coldest month of -10.3 °C (table 5). Monthly average temperatures are milder: -2.5 °C in January, -2.8 °C in February (table 1); these are averages, but it is the coldest days that are dangerous for a pipe.

An outdoor tap on a wall is shut off on the warm side of the house before frost and drained. If a tap has to be built anew, the shut-off valve is placed indoors and the outdoor section is left able to drain. If a pipe has frozen anyway, it is thawed gradually and then checked for a split; the job is described on the page Frozen pipes.

Water source: Dalbe and the neighbouring systems

In the Jelgavas novada KU drinking water testing list, three systems are named for Cenu parish: one shared by Āne and Tetele, and one each for Brankas and Jaunpēternieki. Dalbe is not named in this list. Whether your street has a centralised water supply is checked by address by the company’s environmental engineer.

When a house draws water from its own well or borehole, the plumber’s work starts after the pump: the yard pipe, the service pipe into the house, the pipes to the taps and the water heater. The route from the well to the house is exactly where the LBN rows matter in practice.

The Misa, polders and a village 3–5 m above sea level

The Misa (L129) is on the list of rivers at flood risk in the Lielupe basin, also because of the polders. Dalbe is 3–5 m above sea level, covers 0.943 km², and in 2021 had 216 residents. In 1962 the territory of the “Mičurinietis” collective farm of Dalbe village was added to Cenu village.

For a house closer to the river, before digging a deep route it is worth finding out how high the groundwater stands in spring, because laying and backfilling a pipe in a wet trench takes more work. For a particular plot, ask Jelgava Municipality.

Questions people ask

How deep should the water pipe from the well to the house lie in Dalbe?

For Jelgava, LBN 003-19 gives 91 cm in clayey soil if you assume a frost risk of once in 10 years, and 119 cm once in 100 years; in sand the values are greater. The final depth is set by the soil in the yard and where the service pipe enters the house.

Does Dalbe have the centralised water supply of Jelgavas novada KU?

In the JNKU testing list for Cenu parish, the systems of Āne and Tetele, Brankas and Jaunpēternieki are named, and Dalbe is not. A particular address is checked by the JNKU environmental engineer.

An outdoor tap on a wall burst in winter. What should replace it?

A tap with a shut-off valve on the warm side of the house and a way to drain the outdoor section. The price of an outdoor tap through the wall depends on the amount of work, access and materials; you find out on the call before the job whether the trip to Dalbe is charged separately.
