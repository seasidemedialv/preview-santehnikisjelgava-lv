The Jelgava Municipality environmental report says that the effects of a rising river level are felt especially in Kalnciems Parish, so in home plumbing here the basement comes first, along with where the wastewater goes at high water.

Chronicle: 2010, 2013, 2024 and 2026

  1. 2010, 2.61 m LAS Because of a heavy ice run, the Lielupe level at Kalnciems reached 2.61 m LAS. According to LVĢMC data, the Kalnciems area begins to flood when the level reaches 1.96 m LAS.
  2. 2013 In the spring floods, the 1.96 m LAS mark was exceeded in 2013 too.
  3. July 2024, 180 mm After the downpour, the largest amount of precipitation in the country was recorded in Kalnciems Parish, 180 mm, or 203% of the July norm; allotments and properties flooded in almost the whole parish, basements in particular.
  4. March 2026 The head of the Jelgava Municipality council named the Lielupe on the Kalnciems and Staļģene side among the most vulnerable places before the spring floods.

The chronicle shows that a basement in Kalnciems can take in water in two ways: from the river in spring and from the rain in summer. In both cases the plumbing question is the same: what happens to the house’s wastewater when there is a lot of water around.

Two dykes: 6.2 km and 2.7 km

Kalnciems is protected from the Lielupe by the Kalnciems polder dyke (6.2 km) and the Kalnciems Lielupes iela protective dyke (2.7 km). The largest water-covered areas in Jelgava Municipality are in Kalnciems Parish, 11% of the parish area, and the parish also has the nature reserve “Kalnciema pļavas”.

On LVĢMC maps, the Kalnciems wastewater treatment plant and the Kalnciems freshwater intake lie in the flood-prone area of the Lielupe flood plain polders. Jelgavas novada KU can say how the village networks cope at high water.

A basement below the street sewer: pumping

If a basement has a shower, washing machine, sink or toilet lower than the street sewer branch, the wastewater does not flow to the street by itself. A sewage (lifting) pump with a tank lifts it, and the pressure pipe from it usually runs higher than the street level before joining the house outlet.

In Kalnciems it is important to tell two pumps apart. A drainage pump pumps groundwater out of a pit, while a sewage pump pumps the house’s wastewater. If the basement fixtures are lower than the street branch, their outlet should not be left to gravity alone; it is connected to the lifting pump.

For fitting a sewage (lifting) pump, the tank’s place is chosen so that it can be reached for servicing, and the electrical connection is placed higher than the level to which water can collect in the basement. The fitting price depends on the amount of work, access and materials; an urgent call-out in an emergency is from €60. If there is a trip charge to Kalnciems, the plumber states it on the call before you agree on the job. We describe pump repair on the page Sewage pump repair, and the whole basement topic on the page Drainage and basement pumps.

Village networks and the former town's buildings

In the environmental report of the municipality’s spatial plan, the capacity of the Kalnciems biological treatment plant is given as 325 m³ a day, renewed in 2013. In 2016 the Kalnciems water supply system supplied 129 m³ of water a day to about 2,000 consumers, and the water is treated before supply.

Kalnciems grew up as a large building materials industry centre because of its clay and dolomite deposits, and from 1991 to 2010 it was a town. In early 2010 Kalnciems Parish was formed from the former town and a rural territory, and in 2026 it had 1,767 residents.

Kalnciems also has district heating from Jelgavas novada KU. In houses connected to it, the plumber’s work begins downstream of the heat substation: radiators, risers and checking them; a heating system check and service is from €40 per call-out. In houses with their own heating and water heater, it is worth putting the basement appliances above the level to which water can collect in the basement.

Questions people ask

At what Lielupe level does the Kalnciems area start to flood?

According to LVĢMC data, at 1.96 m LAS. In the spring floods this mark was exceeded in 2010 and 2013, and in 2010, because of the ice run, the level reached 2.61 m LAS.

In a Kalnciems house basement the washing machine is lower than the street branch. What pump does it need?

A sewage (lifting) pump with a tank, not a drainage pump: a drainage pump is meant for groundwater, while the washing machine’s water is wastewater. The fitting price depends on the amount of work, access and materials.

A Kalnciems basement has a sewage pump. Where should its electrical connection go?

Higher than the level to which water can collect in the basement, for example after the July 2024 downpour. The tank is placed so that it can be reached for servicing, and we describe pump repair on the page about sewage pumps.
