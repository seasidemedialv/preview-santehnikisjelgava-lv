Plumber in Līnijas - straight streets in north-west Jelgava

Six numbered lines in north-west Jelgava run from south to north on the left bank of the Lielupe, and in the south the house district is bounded by Dobeles šoseja.

Two new detailed plans here set flood conditions, so new-build plumbing is worth planning together with the floor height.

5. līnija 31: floor above the 10% flood level

In the explanatory report of the detailed plan, the land unit at 5. līnija 31 by Bumbieru ceļš is, by the LVĢMC maps, in a flood-risk area with a 10% probability, although the city’s plan does not class it as a flood-prone area. The ground surface is about 3.00 m LAS, and the area has a system of open ditches.

The detailed plan states that the ground floor of homes must be above the flood level with a 10% probability. In plumbing practice this can be met by putting the water heater, the heating manifolds and the pump controls on that floor, not in a pit or a technical basement.

A ground surface of about 3.00 m LAS is lower than what is usually quoted for the whole city: Jelgava lies on a plain only about 3.5 m above sea level. So on such a plot the space under the floor would be where water collects first, and nothing that runs on electricity is worth putting there.

6. līnija 64C: flood-resistant materials and own heat supply

In the detailed plan for 6. līnija 64C, part of the area is in a flood-risk area, so each land unit must provide measures and building materials for the safe use of buildings in a flood. Heating there is to be solved separately on each land unit, and heat pumps and solar energy solutions are recommended.

For a heat pump, low-temperature heating is usually chosen, for example a water underfloor heating system. The plumber’s part is the floor loops, the manifold and their connection; the unit itself and its settings are usually done by its installer. More on underfloor heating on the page Water underfloor heating installation.

Dobeles šoseja: section 23 in 2020

In section 23 of phase V, on Dobeles šoseja and on Pūra ceļš, Kūliņu ceļš and Ķiršu ceļš, the water main and sewer were put into operation in the first quarter of 2020. Līnijas is counted as 20 streets, including 1. to 6. līnija, Agroķīmiķu, Arāju and Kooperatīva iela, and Šūmaņu ceļš.

For a new build, the availability of a street network has to be established before the design, because it decides whether the house needs its own sewage system. That is done at the Technical Department of SIA Jelgavas ūdens. If there is a network in the street, technical conditions are requested for the connection; they are valid for 2 years, so it is worth requesting them when construction really approaches.

197 heating days and your own sewage system if there is no network

For heat pump and underfloor heating calculations, the climate figure for Jelgava from LBN 003-19 (table 7) is suitable: the heating period lasts 197 days with an average air temperature of 0.7 °C, in Riga 192 days. That is the designer’s input; the plumber builds the loops and the manifold according to the design, and checks the flow in each loop after installation.

If no street sewer is available to a new build, the house needs its own system, and within one month of the start of use it must be registered in the city’s register of decentralised systems; what has to be registered there we explain on the page Plumber in Būriņu dārzi. The internal outlet is then routed so that a later change-over to the street network does not require the floor to be opened.

New-build internal plumbing point by point

In a new build, work is counted in points: each place where water or drainage connects is one point. New-build internal plumbing costs from €55 per outlet. For example, a house with a kitchen, bathroom, toilet, washing machine and utility room can come to 8 points, and then the work is at least €440 (8 × €55), materials extra.

Adjusting or repairing an underfloor heating manifold is priced by the amount of work, access and materials. All new-build work is described on the page Plumbing installation in a house.

Questions people ask

The detailed plan for 5. līnija 31 says the floor must be above the flood level. Where does the water heater go, then?

The plan sets the floor height, not where equipment goes. In practice the water heater and the heating manifolds are put on this floor, so that they are above the flood level with a 10% probability, not in a basement or a pit.

A house at 6. līnija 64C will be heated by a heat pump. What of that is the plumber's work?

The underfloor heating loops, the manifold, radiators if there are any, and the water and drain points. Installing the heat pump unit and its settings are usually done by its installer.

There are open ditches by Bumbieru ceļš. Does that change the choice of sewage system?

The ditches are a land drainage system, not a sewer. Whether a street sewer network is available to the plot is established at the Technical Department of Jelgavas ūdens; it decides whether the house needs its own system.
