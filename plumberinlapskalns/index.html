Plumber in Lapskalns - drains to Lapskalna iela 22

Jelgava’s municipal wastewater treatment plant is at Lapskalna iela 22, and if a building is connected to the city sewer, its wastewater ends up there.

Lapskalns is a large district in the north of Jelgava, on the left bank of the Driksa, where blocks of flats, detached houses, allotments and an airfield took shape as former fields became part of the city.

The basement of a building on Meiju ceļš

Jelgava has four blocks of flats of the 464 series, and three of them are on Meiju ceļš: No. 14, 16 and 20. Buildings of this series have five storeys above ground and one below. The buildings surveyed in a Ministry of Economics study (not every address) were commissioned in 1964–1978.

In the below-ground storey the flats' soil stacks join a horizontal drain that leaves the building. If the blockage is in that drain, water rises first in the sinks and baths of the lowest floors while the upper floors still drain. So a repeated blockage on the ground floor is a reason to look at the basement drain.

Our reading of the map (OpenStreetMap): more than ten blocks of flats are marked on Meiju ceļš No. 14–50. Only three of them are of the 464 series, but a similar basement drain can be in others.

The new sewer on Meiju ceļš: from Audēju iela to No. 6a

In section 13 of phase V, a sewer was built on Meiju ceļš, on the stretch from Audēju iela to Meiju ceļš 6a, and the works were completed at the end of 2020. Between a building’s outlet and this sewer there is a yard section for which the owner is responsible; in a block of flats it is usually common property, unless the building’s documents say otherwise.

This is one section of street sewer, not a map of the whole street. Wastewater from a particular building on Meiju ceļš can go along this or another street sewer; the exact route by address is known to Jelgavas ūdens.

A flat city: 32 pumping stations

Jelgava is flat, so 32 sewage pumping stations have been built for the sewer to work, and all of them run automatically. One of the sections completed in phase V leads straight to Lapskalns: in 2019, in section 32, the sewer network from Zvejnieku iela No. 10 to Lapskalna iela No. 22 was completed.

From Lapskalns the pressure main goes on to the right bank of the Lielupe; more on that on the page Plumber in Salas. Of this whole system the house owner sees only their own part: traps, soil stacks, the horizontal drain and the yard drain to the street.

Screens with 3 mm gaps at Lapskalna iela 22

In the mechanical stage of the treatment plant, automatic screens with 3 mm gaps remove undissolved matter: rags, sand, grease. The plant receives 9–12 thousand m³ of wastewater a day, and the treated water is discharged into the Driksa.

Everything the screens catch has first passed through someone’s trap, the building’s horizontal drain and the yard drain. Regulations No. 19-10 (points 34.2 and 34.3) prohibit discharging into the sewer substances that block networks: solid objects, textiles, sand, plaster, oils, grease and unground food waste.

The airfield polder in the north

Lapskalns also has an airfield, and around it on the left bank of the Lielupe lies the Jelgava airfield polder. The flood protection plan lists the reconstruction of the airfield polder dyke to prevent flood danger as a completed project; the total length of the polder’s protective dykes is 5.1 km. In the 2010 and 2013 floods the Lielupe level upstream of the polder exceeded the danger mark by 28–84 cm.

The 2022–2027 plan states that wastewater treatment plants and water intake sites are outside flood-risk areas (in the 2015 plan, the Jelgavas ūdens wastewater plant was among three plants in the zone with a 10% flood probability). For detached houses and allotments around the airfield, what matters most on the house side is the owner’s own outlet and, if there is one, a basement pump.

Jetting the basement horizontal drain

If grease and sediment have built up in the horizontal drain, they are flushed out with a high-pressure jet through a cleaning hatch. Jetting costs from €60 per hour, minimum 1 hour; for example, two hours of work is at least €120 (2 × €60). The method is described on the page Sewer jetting in Jelgava.

If the blockage is in a single flat, clearing a blockage from €40 is enough. If the soil stack itself is blocked, clearing it starts from €80.

Questions people ask

What from a Meiju ceļš building's wastewater is left on the 3 mm screens at Lapskalna iela 22?

The screens remove undissolved matter such as rags, sand and grease. Regulations No. 19-10 prohibit discharging the same substances into the sewer because they block networks, and the first pipe they block is usually the building’s horizontal drain.

In a Meiju ceļš building a blockage keeps coming back on the ground floor. Could the basement drain be to blame?

It could. Buildings of the 464 series have a below-ground storey where the soil stacks join a horizontal drain. If it is partly blocked, water rises first on the lowest floors. A look shows whether one flat or the horizontal drain has to be cleared.

Can a Meiju ceļš building's horizontal drain be jetted without digging?

Yes, if the drain has a cleaning hatch: the jet hose is fed in through it. High-pressure jetting is from €60 per hour, minimum 1 hour; two hours of work is at least €120.
