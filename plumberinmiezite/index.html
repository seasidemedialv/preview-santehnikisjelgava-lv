March: signs on Miezītes ceļš

On 12 March 2026 warning signs about a flooded carriageway were put up on Miezītes ceļš, and the same report mentions that neighbouring Bāra ceļš flooded. That is news about the carriageway, not about houses; no data on the basements of particular houses has been published for Miezīte.

In spring it is worth looking into the inspection chamber in the yard: if water stands in it above the pipe, the drain empties more slowly, and the cause can be either an overfull street network or sediment in the yard drain itself.

April: drainage water and the new outlet

In spring the temptation is simple: to put the hose of a basement or foundation drainage pump into the nearest sewer inspection chamber. Jelgava binding regulations No. 19-10, point 15.7, prohibit it: rainwater and groundwater may not be discharged into the centralised sewer at will.

Jelgavas ūdens states that it can calculate and recover compensation if a drainage system has been connected to the user’s outlet without construction documentation. The amount of compensation depends on the inner diameter of the pipe, so a wider connection costs more.

Jelgava binding regulations No. 19-10, point 15.7, and the Jelgavas ūdens compensation procedure, checked 19 November 2026.

Manufacturers have their own requirements for the discharge pipe. For example, the manual for the Wilo Drain TMW 32 requires the discharge pipe to be installed frost-protected, with a gate valve and a backflow valve, and prohibits running the pump without liquid. We describe pump repair on the page Drainage pump repair in Jelgava.

If a house was connected soon after the network was built, its yard drain is as new as the street network. The first springs are when you see whether the pipe was laid with an even fall and whether sand is collecting in the inspection chambers.

May: checking the yard drain before the next floods

Once the water has receded, the yard drain can be cleared and looked at. Clearing the yard drain line costs from €50 per call-out (1st hour). If the drain has to be relaid or extended, laying pipe starts from €10/m, materials extra.

If water collects in the basement every spring, the pump’s place and its discharge have to be planned together: a pit, a pump and a discharge pipe to a place that is not the domestic sewer. The price of fitting a drainage pump depends on the amount of work, access and materials. Everything about connecting to the street network is on the page Sewer and water connection for a house.

July and November: a downpour, and the pump in winter

Spring is not the only season. On 30 July 2024 LSM wrote that after a downpour in Jelgava the yards, cellars and foundations of many private houses were still under water and pumping did not help in many places, because there was nowhere to pump to. That is a description at city level, not a list of Miezīte’s streets, but it shows that a pump’s discharge needs a place that accepts water.

In autumn the pump is prepared for winter. Whether it may stay in the pit is decided by the manufacturer’s manual and by how warm the basement is in winter; for an example with a specific model, see the questions at the end of the page.

Questions people ask

Can a drainage pump hose be put into the yard sewer inspection chamber during floods?

No. Point 15.7 of Regulations No. 19-10 prohibits discharging groundwater and rainwater into the domestic sewer at will, and Jelgavas ūdens can calculate compensation by the pipe’s diameter. The pump’s discharge needs another, agreed place.

A drainage pump stays in the basement pit in autumn. What has to be done for it to get through winter?

It depends on the model. For example, the Wilo Drain TMW 32 manual requires a pump that stays installed to be fully submerged in liquid, with the ambient and liquid temperature at least +3 °C. If the pump is not used for a long time, one pumping cycle is run every 2 months.

A house on Siena ceļš was connected in 2019. What should be checked in the yard drain after spring?

Whether the inspection chambers have no sand or standing water, and whether the water leaves the house without delay. If in doubt, the drain is looked at with a camera. The owner is responsible for their own yard system (Regulations No. 19-10, point 20).
