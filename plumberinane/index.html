Āne is on the right bank of the Lielupe between the river, the A8 road and the P93 road, about 2 m above sea level, and its homes get water from a system shared with Tetele.

A terraced house with sections: an inlet for each owner

The detailed plan for Celtnieku iela 11B in Āne, approved on 29 November 2023, provides for detached houses and also terraced houses. A terraced house raises one plumbing question that a detached house does not: how to divide water and sewerage between the sections.

A terraced house can be divided into sections with different owners. Then each section should have its own inlet, or at least its own meter and its own shut-off, so that work in one section does not mean cutting off a neighbour’s water. This is provided for in the design, before the walls are closed. The same goes for sewerage: if the sections' outlets are connected in one line, a clog at one end affects all the sections behind it.

What the municipal co-funding covers and what it does not

Jelgava Municipality co-funds connecting homes to the centralised sewer (binding regulations No. 26, 27 July 2022). The table shows what the regulations say about each part of the costs:

Part of the costs Co-funding Note
Connection to the centralised sewer half of the costs, but no more than €1,500 for homes
Households in poverty or on low income, and others can cover all connection costs by the criteria in the regulations
The building’s internal sewerage not included the owner pays
Rainwater drainage not included the owner pays

The ceiling arithmetic is simple: if the connection costs €2,400, half is €1,200, which fits within the €1,500 limit. If the costs are €3,600, half would be €1,800, but co-funding stops at €1,500. Regulations No. 26 speak about connection to the centralised sewer, so the cost of a water supply connection is worth finding out separately.

Jelgava Municipality binding regulations No. 26 (27 July 2022), likumi.lv, checked 19 November 2026.

The inside of the house, which the co-funding does not cover

A plumber’s work during a connection is mostly exactly the part the regulations do not include: switching the outlet over in the house, new connection points and old outlet sections in the house that need renewing. An example for a detached house: the outlet is switched over at two connection points, 2 × from €35, at least €70 in total. If the old outlet section in the basement up to the outer wall also needs renewing, its scope is assessed on site and it is the house owner’s expense.

Āne also has 13 blocks of flats. In a flat, renewing a drain branch is from €30 per outlet, while the shared soil stack is usually decided by the flat owners or the building manager. That is the labour price, materials extra; if the trip to Āne is charged on top of the work, the plumber says so on the call before the job starts. We describe the course of a connection on the page Sewer and water connection for a private house.

A brick village by the quarry waters

The village grew around the Āne manor (Aahof) and later became one of the centres of Latvia’s brick industry; the centre of the Spartaks building ceramics factory was in Āne. Near the village are five bodies of water, quarries and lakes, including the Senču, Ānes and Jāņa ponds and Sudrablīcis.

A low village between the river and the quarry waters needs one check before a new build: where the groundwater is on the plot. If a house is planned with a basement or a utility room below ground level, this decides whether wastewater from there can flow to the street on its own or will need to be pumped. It is cheaper to make this decision in the design than after the first spring.

Āne covers 1.89 km², and in 2025 it had 1,312 residents. Cenu parish has no administration of its own; the service centres are in Brankas and Āne, and the pre-school is in Āne, but the nearest primary school is in Tetele.

Questions people ask

Who takes over the Jānīši water pipe once it is built?

The Jānīši detailed plan states that the water supply networks built in the Āne village territory are handed over to Jelgavas novada KU. After that this company is responsible for the street network, and the owner for the home’s internal system.

Children from Āne go to school in Tetele. Does Tetele have the same tap water?

Yes. Jelgavas novada KU runs one water supply system for both villages, Āne and Tetele, so the tap at the school in Tetele and the tap at home in Āne give the same water.

Can a family in poverty get the whole connection sum?

Municipality regulations No. 26 provide that for households in poverty or on low income and some other groups, co-funding can cover all connection costs. Internal sewerage and rainwater drainage are not part of it.
