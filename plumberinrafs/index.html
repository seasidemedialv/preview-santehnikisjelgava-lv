Plumber in Rafs - RAF workers' blocks of flats and the Vecais ceļš gardens

The blocks of flats for workers of the Riga Bus Factory, between Pērnavas iela and Loka maģistrāle on the right bank of the Lielupe, form the core of Rafs, while the Vecais ceļš street blocks have family houses with gardens.

What the Ministry of Economics found at Pērnavas iela 2 and Loka maģistrāle 13

Blocks of flats of the 602 series (1Лг-602Р) were also built in Jelgava, and some of the last buildings of this series in the city were put up in the early 1990s. The Ministry of Economics study surveyed Jelgava buildings at Pērnavas iela 2, Pērnavas iela 32, Vecais ceļš 32 and Loka maģistrāle 13.

The survey at Pērnavas iela 2 and Loka maģistrāle 13 found that the cement mortar filling of the joints between panels had lost its bond with the panels and in many places cracks and crumbles. That is a finding about the structure, not about plumbing. For plumbing it means one thing: if a building is being prepared for renovation, the question of risers and the flats' pipes has to be raised in the same project. How to plan it is explained on the page What changes in a flat’s plumbing when the building is renovated.

1975–1998: the factory on Aviācijas iela and the school of 1978

The Riga Bus Factory moved into new buildings in Jelgava, at Aviācijas iela 18, and operated there from 1975 to 1998. Jelgava Secondary School No. 6, now Pārlielupe Primary School, opened on Loka maģistrāle in 1978, when the factory workers' residential buildings were being built. An LLU study describes a 25 ha arc of high-rise buildings along the Ozolnieki forest plot, with a school and a shopping centre.

Our reading of the map (OpenStreetMap): nine-storey buildings are marked on Loka maģistrāle, at Pērnavas iela 2 and 32 and at Vecais ceļš 32. In a building like this the riser usually runs through all the floors, so any work on it is a matter for the whole building, while the inside of a flat, including the water heater, is the owner’s decision.

A water test from the Pārlielupe primary school tap

In the BIOR test ordered by Jelgavas ūdens, the sample of 15 September 2026 was taken from a cold water tap at Pārlielupe Primary School. It is one tap on one day, not a measurement of the whole district, but it shows what the water in the city network was like at that place at that moment.

Manufacturers give water requirements for their own model. For example, Bosch Tronic 2000T requires hardness of at least 120 ppm (7.2 °dH), pH 6.5–9.5 and conductivity of 130–1500 µS/cm; the school test’s conductivity and pH are within these limits. More on hardness on the page How hard is Jelgava’s water, and what does it mean for a water heater?.

A water heater in the bathroom of a nine-storey block

The manufacturer sets the rules for the place. The Bosch Tronic 2000T manual requires the water heater to be placed outside bathroom protection zones 1 and 2, more than 60 cm from the bath, in a room where the temperature does not fall below 0 °C, and so that the anode can be replaced.

Water heater fitting or swap of the old one costs from €60 each, a water heater swap including removal of the old one from €80 each; why there are two lines, we explain in the questions at the end of the page. The work is described on the page Water heater installation and repair in Jelgava.

The Vecais ceļš street basin: sections 19 and 27

Most of the district is the Vecais ceļš street “basin” with family houses, gardens and sports facilities. In section 19 of phase V, on Avotu, Stadiona, Robežu and Lauksaimnieku iela, the water supply and sewer were handed over at the end of 2018, in section 27, on Kameņu, Aroniju, Strazdu and Rīgas iela, in the third quarter of 2020. Section 1 of the 2015 procurement provided for networks on Druvu iela.

Questions people ask

I want to put a water heater in the bathroom of a nine-storey block in Rafs. What does the manufacturer require?

It depends on the model. For example, Bosch Tronic 2000T has to go outside protection zones 1 and 2, more than 60 cm from the bath, in a room not below 0 °C and so that the anode can be replaced.

Does the Pārlielupe primary school water test apply to my flat on Pērnavas iela?

The test shows the water at one tap on 15 September 2026: conductivity 742 µS/cm, sulphates 221 mg/l, pH 7.4, in city network water at that point; it is not a measurement of your flat. Jelgavas ūdens publishes test reports every month.

Why does water heater fitting start from €60, but a swap with removal of the old one from €80?

The first line covers putting the water heater in place and connecting it, the second a job in which the old water heater first has to be taken off and disconnected. Which line applies to your flat, you find out on the call when you go through the job and the price; it is the labour price, materials extra.
