Ābelītes, Atpūtas, Kalmju and five more streets

Lediņi is counted as eight streets: Ābelītes, Atpūtas, Kalmju, Kalna, Palejas, Vircavas and Ziedu streets and Lediņu ceļš. It is an allotment district, and it also has Jelgava’s highest point, Lediņu kalns, which is nowadays overgrown with trees.

Lediņi streets are not mentioned in Jelgavas ūdens phase V section reports. That does not mean no address has a connection, but it does mean that for a particular plot you ask Jelgavas ūdens, rather than judging by a neighbour.

If a plot has its own sewage system, a dry toilet or a holding tank, it has to be registered in the city’s register; what exactly must be registered and by when, we explain on the page Plumber in Būriņu dārzi.

Vircava: the city boundary and the river's depth

Lediņi joins Jelgava’s eastern boundary along the Vircava, a tributary on the left bank of the Lielupe that flows into the Lielupe in the south-east of the city, at Sieramuiža. The Lielupe flood protection plan lists “restoring the original design depth of the Vircava river” as a completed ZMNĪ measure.

In plumbing a boundary also means a different service provider. Lediņi is in the city, so Jelgava’s city regulations and Jelgavas ūdens apply here. To the south of Lediņi is already Jelgava Municipality, where village networks are served by SIA Jelgavas novada KU; we write about them on the pages Plumber in Mežciems and Plumber in Kārniņi.

An unheated garden house from November to March

An allotment house often stands empty in winter. If it is not heated, the water left in pipes, taps, traps and hoses can burst a pipe or push out a seal in a frost. So before the frost the water is drained from the whole system, including the outdoor tap.

For a water heater the rules come from its manual. If the manufacturer requires a room where the temperature does not fall below 0 °C, then in an unheated garden house the water heater is disconnected from the electricity and drained before the frost, and in spring it is first filled and only then switched on.

The whole sequence is described on the page Winterising a garden house or summer cottage water system. The price of draining for winter and opening the system in spring depends on the amount of work, access and materials.

The first filling after winter

In spring the system is filled slowly with the taps closed, and then the taps are opened one at a time to let the air out. At this moment you see whether any joint burst over winter, and it is better to see that while there is still someone in the house who can close the supply.

Stopping a drip or leak point is from €40 per job. For example, if two separate joints leak after winter, the work is at least €80 (2 × €40), materials extra. For small seasonal jobs the hourly rate from €25/h can also be used.

If a pipe has burst in a wall or under the floor over winter, the damaged section is renewed: repairing a pipe section is from €50 per job. The first places to look are sections along an outer wall and under an unheated floor.

Sieramuiža-Lediņi: a district on paper

In the residents' council regulations (2026), Lediņi belongs to the district Sieramuiža-Lediņi, which lies between the left bank of the Lielupe, the city’s eastern boundary along the Vircava, Lediņu ceļš, Bauskas iela and the river Platone. The regulations state that this division is used only for council elections and does not change any other territorial division. The two parts have Vircava in common: Lediņi joins it along the boundary, while in Sieramuiža it flows into the Lielupe.

In plumbing this means that the neighbouring Sieramuiža networks on Dzirnavu iela prove nothing about the Lediņi plots: a connection, contract and technical conditions are looked at by Jelgavas ūdens by address. For the streets of Sieramuiža see more on the page Plumber in Sieramuiža.

Questions people ask

In spring, when water is turned on in a Lediņi garden house, a joint leaks. What to do first?

Close the supply and do not open it until the joint is repaired; if water has reached the floor, disconnect the electrical appliances in that room. Stopping a drip or leak point is from €40 per job, materials extra.

The coldest five days in Jelgava average -21.7 °C. Do the traps in a garden house also need draining?

Yes, if the house is not heated. A trap usually holds water, and when it freezes it can crack the plastic or push out a seal. The trap is emptied or removed, and this is done together with draining the taps and hoses.

Lediņi is officially in the district Sieramuiža-Lediņi. Does that change who you sign the water contract with?

No. The division into districts is used only for the residents' council elections. The contract is signed with Jelgavas ūdens, and the company looks at the address, not the district name.
