Plumber in Tetele - one water main with Āne, outside the JNKU heating list

Āne and Tetele are neighbouring villages in Cenu parish on the Lielupe bank, with a quarry between them, and they have one water supply system.

Tetele lies about 7 km from the centre of Jelgava (our map reading), yet the village is absent from the Jelgavas novada KU list of centralised heating, so hot water in Tetele houses is usually up to the house’s own water heater or heating boiler.

Tetele covers 1.46 km², lies 3–5 m above sea level, and in 2021 had 353 residents. In water matters the two villages are one: the same service provider and the same municipality regulations No. 2. The difference is behind the meter: if a house is not connected to centralised heating, it heats its own water, and that is where water heater jobs arise. For Āne, JNKU heating tariffs were set by SPRK in 2020, so in the neighbouring village some houses may be connected to district heat, and a neighbour’s solution in Āne does not always suit a house in Tetele.

The Tetele well field for the city, a shared system for the village

The name Tetele also appears in Jelgava’s water supply. Jelgavas ūdens history notes that in 1975 the Tetele pumping station with 10 artesian wells was handed over, and since January 2014 the city has been supplied from the Tetele well field with 9 new and 3 rebuilt wells.

For a tap in the village of Tetele this is a different route. The village’s houses get water from the Āne and Tetele water supply system of Jelgavas novada KU, and its analyses are published by JNKU, not by Jelgavas ūdens. The city’s water figures, such as the sulphate content after nanofiltration, do not apply to the village.

A water heater in a house without centralised heating

Jelgavas novada KU states that heating hard water forms limescale, which can damage plumbing and heating elements. How fast that happens in a particular water heater is seen by opening the tank: if the limescale layer is thick, the next service is planned sooner.

What exactly the service involves and how often the anode must be checked is set by the water heater’s manufacturer in its instructions, and the requirements differ between models. So before a service it is worth finding the water heater’s model and its instructions, rather than going by a neighbour’s water heater.

The safety group at the water heater’s cold water inlet may let out a little water during heating. If it drips continuously even when the water is not being heated, it is looked at together with the tank, because the cause can be limescale in the valve itself.

Descaling a water heater and checking the anode is from €50 each, a water heater element, anode or thermostat swap from €40 per job. That is the labour price, materials extra; whether a new anode or seal is needed is seen once the tank is opened. The work is described on the page Water heater descaling. If the old water heater can no longer be repaired, water heater fitting or swap of the old one is from €60 each, and a water heater swap including removal of the old one from €80 each.

The manor park, the brickworks and the Lielupe flood plain

The village of Tetele grew up in the 19th century as a brick-making centre; in 1903 the manor brickworks employed 130 workers. The former manor’s lookout tower (around 1840) is on the list of State protected cultural monuments, and by Tetele Primary School a manor park with a small tower has survived.

Part of the “Lielupes palienes pļavas” nature reserve lies in Cenu parish, and in the LVĢMC flood risk plan the flood plain polders with this reserve are one of the nationally significant flood risk areas of the Lielupe basin. Whether a particular plot touches the flood plain, ask the local government before choosing a place for a basement water heater or pump.

Questions people ask

Where can I find the tap water analyses for Tetele?

In the Jelgavas novada KU drinking water test reports, under the Āne and Tetele water supply system. If the analyses lack an indicator you need, such as hardness, you can ask the company itself.

A Tetele house has no centralised heating and limescale builds up in the water heater. Where to start?

By opening and cleaning the tank: descaling a water heater and checking the anode is from €50 each. The hardness figure for the Āne and Tetele system can be asked of Jelgavas novada KU, and it helps decide how often to repeat the service.

Does the Lielupe flood plain meadows reserve affect Tetele houses?

The reserve lies partly in Cenu parish, and LVĢMC assesses the flood plain polders with it as a nationally significant flood risk area. For a particular plot, information is given by Jelgava Municipality.
